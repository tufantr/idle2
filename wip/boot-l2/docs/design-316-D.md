
**What the world did meanwhile** (P-127). The report covers the hero; after a day or more away a strip under its haul
covers the world, a line each and only for what the player has met (`src/systems/reentry.js`, opened by the disclosure
`reentry`: the clan, the weekend events or the Trials): the clan's week (levels of its boss felled and the banner they
raise, as the server last said it this week), the weekend event running or the last one if it ran while away (with the
Festival Tokens won meanwhile), the week's Trial and its laurel, and what the new version changed (a hook for a changelog,
empty until one fills it). Each line is the latest of its kind, so a month away reads as short as a day. With none of
them, and nothing ready on the report either, one line says nothing is waiting. The clan's and the event's lines go to
their tabs.
