## Key question 1: How do successful idle games balance active vs idle play, how big and how long are active buffs, and what precedents exist for skill mini-games?

### Takeaway
The genre spans two extremes: Cookie Clicker makes active play a large, bursty multiplier (Frenzy ×7 for 77 s, Click Frenzy ×777 for 13 s, spawning every 5–15 min at base), Clicker Heroes makes *idle* a build with its own multipliers that only switch on after 60 s of no clicking, and Melvor/Antimatter Dimensions offer no active production bonus at all. Mini-game precedents (IdleOn, RuneScape random events) show that timed/gated attempts (daily plays or cooldowns) keep active play optional, and RuneScape eventually removed its forced mini-events because they annoyed legitimate players.

### Cited Findings
**Cookie Clicker (primary source: game code)**
- Golden cookies spawn on a timer whose base window is 5 to 15 minutes: `getMinTime` returns `m=5` and `getMaxTime` returns `m=15` (minutes) before modifiers — [ozh/cookieclicker main.js L5982-5991](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). The spawn roll each frame after `minTime` is `Math.random()<Math.pow((me.time-me.minTime)/(me.maxTime-me.minTime),5)` (L5502), so spawns cluster toward the end of the window.
- Spawn frequency upgrades: `if (Game.Has('Lucky day')) m/=2; if (Game.Has('Serendipity')) m/=2;` plus several ×0.95/×0.99 modifiers (`Golden goose egg`, `Heavenly luck`, `Green yeast digestives`) — [main.js L5936-5945](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). A wiki summary states that with Lucky Day and Serendipity a golden cookie appears on average every 4 min 50 s, and at high progression roughly every 2 minutes — [Cookie Clicker Golden Cookie wiki](https://cookieclicker.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Cookie) (search snippet; page blocked).
- A golden cookie stays on screen 13 s (`me.dur=13;//duration; the cookie's lifespan in seconds before it despawns`, L5608), doubled by each of Lucky day and Serendipity (`dur*=2`, L5611-5612) — [main.js](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js).
- Effect sizes and durations (seconds × `effectDurMod`, multiplier): `frenzy` 77 s ×7 CpS; `click frenzy` 13 s ×777 click power; `dragon harvest` 60 s ×15; `dragonflight` 10 s ×1111 clicks; `blood frenzy` (Elder Frenzy) 6 s ×666; `clot` 66 s ×0.5; `cursed finger` 10 s; `cookie storm` 7 s; `everything must go` 8 s ×5 — [main.js L5777-5848](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). `Get lucky` doubles all effect durations (`effectDurMod*=2`, L5713).
- "Lucky" payout: `mult*Math.min(Game.cookies*0.15,Game.cookiesPs*60*15)+13` — i.e., the lesser of 15% of banked cookies or 15 minutes of production, plus 13 — [main.js L5789](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js).
- Outcome selection builds a candidate list: base `['frenzy','multiply cookies']`; 10% chance to add `click frenzy`; 25% chance to add `building special` once ≥10 buildings; 3% chance (after 100,000 cookies earned) to add `chain cookie`/`cookie storm`; 0.05% `free sugar lump`; and an 80% chance to remove whichever effect fired last so the same buff rarely repeats — [main.js L5670-5698](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js).
- Combo framing from wiki: Frenzy (×7, 77 s) stacked with Click Frenzy (×777, 13 s) gives ×5,439 per click for the overlap — [Cookie Clicker wiki](https://cookieclicker.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Cookie) (search snippet; page blocked).

**Clicker Heroes (calculator source + wiki snippets)**
- Idle mode "by not clicking or activating a skill for 60 seconds, provided you have either Libertas or Siyalatas"; using abilities cancels idle and restarts the 60 s timer — [Clicker Heroes idle DPS guide](https://blog.clickerheroes.com/clicker-heroes-idle-dps-the-ultimate-guide/) and [Clicker Heroes General Tips wiki](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/General_Tips) (search snippets; pages blocked).

By that formula, Siyalatas level 100 gives +2,050% idle DPS.
- Wiki summary: "Level 4 Siyalatas (+100% DPS when idle) and Level 4 Libertas (+100% Gold when idle) will let you earn 4 times as much Gold while idle"; Xyliqil boosts the idle ancients — [Clicker Heroes Builds wiki](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Builds), [Siyalatas wiki](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Siyalatas,_Ancient_of_Abandon) (search snippets; pages blocked).

**Melvor Idle**
- Melvor Idle's design "took the best elements of RuneScape ... stripped them down and put them into an auto form"; there are no quests and the sole focus is progression — [MMORPG.com interview with Brendan Malcolm](https://www.mmorpg.com/interviews/interview-digging-deep-into-melvor-idle-with-its-creator-brendan-malcolm-2000123838) (search snippet; page blocked).

### Inferences
- (Inference from the Cookie Clicker constants) Over an average 10-minute base spawn cycle, a Frenzy (6 extra CpS-multiples for 77 s = 462 CpS-seconds) is worth about +77% of a cycle's idle output when it fires, and Lucky pays up to 15 min of CpS (+150% of a cycle); since roughly half of base spawns are each, a player who catches every golden cookie earns on the order of 2× idle rate early, rising to ~5× once spawn time is quartered by Lucky day + Serendipity. This is the genre's high-end for "active ≈ 2–5× idle".
- (Inference) Clicker Heroes' opposite design — a 60-second "no input" gate before idle multipliers apply — is the cleanest precedent for making a purely idle style *competitive* rather than merely tolerated; its per-level bonus front-loads (+25%/level) and then decays (+15%/level floor).
- (Inference for Fantasy Idle) The current +20–49% for 15–18 s per mini-game success is far below Cookie Clicker's burst sizes but the mini-game cadence is what matters: with no cooldown, a perfect active player can keep the buff up ~100% of the time, making active play mandatory for optimal play (the RuneScape lesson). Recommended: either (a) a golden-cookie-style opportunity model — mini-game prompts appear every 3–8 min, succeed → +50–100% skill speed for 60–120 s, giving a ceiling of roughly 1.5–2× idle for a fully active player — or (b) a daily-attempt budget in the IdleOn style (e.g., 5–10 attempts/day, +4 per upgrade). Add a Clicker Heroes-style "focus" bonus (+10–25%) that turns on after 60 s without input so idle play is never strictly dominated.
- (Inference) The combat click-combo meter (crit/lifesteal/echo) should follow the same rule: cap the uptime (cooldown after a full combo) rather than the per-hit size, so the *expected* combat throughput of an active player stays ≤ ~2× idle.

## Key question 2: What offline-progress rules and caps do idle games use, and should offline be 100% or reduced efficiency?

### Takeaway
Two families exist: "full simulation, capped by time" (Melvor 24 h at 100%, Trimps Time Warp 24 h at 100% then a cheaper "Trustworthy Trimps" fallback, Kittens ~10 in-game years) and "reduced efficiency, capped by time" (Cookie Clicker: 5% of CpS for 1 h then 0.5%, upgradeable to 91% for 7 d 8 h). Antimatter Dimensions caps *resolution* (100,000 ticks, each ≥33 ms and ≤1 day) rather than time, and Evolve gives no offline output at all, instead banking up to 8 hours of accelerated game speed. Reasons documented in the sources are simulation cost/accuracy and daily-check-in retention; anti-exploit pressure shows up in Melvor's reroll-by-reload bug reports.

### Cited Findings
**Melvor Idle**
- "Offline Progression allows the game to progress while closed as if the player had remained online ... up to a maximum of 24 hours", granting Skill XP, Mastery XP, items etc. — [Melvor wiki Offline Progression](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Offline_Progression) (search snippet; page blocked).
- The cap "started at 12 hours and was raised over time to 24 after player feedback", simulated "at 100% of the online rate" — [tideward.app offline progression comparison](https://tideward.app/offline-progression/) (search snippet; page blocked). Corroborating primary evidence: a Nov 2021 bug report on beta v1.0 shows the in-game warning stating an 18-hour maximum and the reporter noting "previously this warning only showed once you were past the max offline time" (12 h) — [MelvorIdle issue #1703](https://github.com/MelvorIdle/melvoridle.github.io/issues/1703); a Steam thread is titled "Offline Progress Maxed at 18 Hours" — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/discussions/0/4665175132461478006/) (title only).
- Combat offline is "safe to idle ... as long as you don't run out of Food, Ammo or Runes"; higher auto-eat tiers with automatic food-slot swapping are recommended before going offline; the offline summary shows approximately how much food is eaten per hour; Thieving offline carries death risk like combat — [Steam "Auto-Eat While Doing Offline Combat?"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/discussions/0/707749927473812598/), [Melvor wiki Offline Progression](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Offline_Progression) (search snippets; pages blocked).
- Offline simulation is a literal tick replay: an Android crash log shows `Game.runTicks` → `Game.tick` → `CombatManager.activeTick` → `Timer.tick` executed "while processing ticks offline" — [MelvorIdle issue #3243](https://github.com/MelvorIdle/melvoridle.github.io/issues/3243).
- Failure modes reported: on Android, locking the device overnight yielded "only 20 to 40 minutes of progress instead of 8 to 9 hours" (background tick loss vs timestamp-based catch-up) — [issue #4740](https://github.com/MelvorIdle/melvoridle.github.io/issues/4740); Adventure-mode offline mining once produced 38% of the expected amount while the standard-mode character was correct — [issue #1126](https://github.com/MelvorIdle/melvoridle.github.io/issues/1126).
- The 24-hour cap can be lifted with the community "Unlimited Offline" mod — [Melvor wiki Offline Progression](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Offline_Progression) (search snippet; page blocked).
- A Steam thread titled "Maximum offline progression - WHY?" exists; its content was blocked — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/discussions/0/3192493998739513526/).

**Cookie Clicker (primary source)**
- Offline production runs only if `Game.mobile || Game.Has('Perfect idling') || Game.Has('Twin Gates of Transcendence')`; base `maxTime=60*60` seconds and `percent=5`; each of seven demon-named heavenly upgrades (Belphegor, Mammon, Abaddon, Satan, Asmodeus, Beelzebub, Lucifer) doubles `maxTime`; each of seven angel-named upgrades (Angels … God) adds +10 percentage points; Chimera adds 2 days and +5; Fern tea +3, Ichor syrup +7, Fortune #102 +1. Then `amount=(timeOfflineOptimal + timeOfflineReduced*0.1) * Game.cookiesPs * (percent/100)` where `timeOfflineOptimal=Math.min(timeOffline,maxTime)` — i.e., time beyond the window counts at one-tenth — [ozh/cookieclicker main.js L3543-3585](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). The welcome notification reports both segments ("X at N% CpS, plus Y at N/10%").
- The in-game upgrade text confirms: "keep making cookies while the game is closed, at the rate of 5% of your regular CpS and up to 1 hour after the game is closed. (Beyond 1 hour, this is reduced by a further 90% - your rate goes down to 0.5% of your CpS.)" — [main.js L10905](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). Wiki totals: maximum 91% offline CpS and 7 days 8 hours of optimal duration — [Cookie Clicker Offline Cookie Production wiki](https://cookieclicker.wiki.gg/wiki/Offline_Cookie_Production) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Optional "sleep mode": if the timeout preference is on and there is no activity for 5 minutes the game pauses to a resume screen (`time-Game.lastActivity>=1000*60*5`) — [main.js L17182, L2052](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js).

**Trimps (primary source)**
- "You can Time Warp for 100% of the time you spent offline, up to a maximum of 24 hours." and `maxTicks: 864000` (24 h × 36,000 ticks/h) — [Trimps main.js L2700, L2865](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/main.js). Four modes: No Offline Progress / Hybrid Offline / Time Warp Only / Trustworthy Trimps Only; "Time Warp caps at 24 hours, so using Hybrid Offline will grant Trustworthy Trimps ... for all offline time over 24 hours" — [Trimps config.js L1774-1780](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/config.js).
- Trustworthy Trimps (the cheap fallback) only credits gatherer jobs (`["Farmer","Lumberjack","Miner","Dragimp","Explorer"]`) for the offline seconds and ignores absences under 60 s — [main.js L3136-3160](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/main.js). Runestones from Trustworthy Trimps were capped to 7 days in v5.9.0; v5.10.0 added a Time Warp speed slider — [Trimps updates.html](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/updates.html).
- Time Warp runs the real game loop at high speed with a user-settable speed limit (`timeWarpLimit`, 50× steps up to unlimited) — [main.js L2711-2727](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/main.js).

**Antimatter Dimensions (primary source)**
- On load: `rawDiff = Date.now() - player.lastUpdate`; nothing is simulated under 10 s; under 50 s a "fast" 50-tick simulation runs; otherwise `simulateTime(diff/1000)` — [AD storage.js L493-511](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/storage.js).
- Tick budget: `maxOfflineTicks = min(offlineTicks option, floor(simulatedMs/33), 1e6)` with the comment "Ticks should never be shorter than 33ms (this would allow offline to exploit tick microstructure)" — [storage.js L83-89](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/storage.js); default `offlineTicks: 1e5`, `offlineProgress: true`, `hibernationCatchup: true` — [player.js L794-799](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/player.js).
- How-to-play text: "Offline tick count can be adjusted between 500 and 1,000,000 ticks ... There is a limit of one day of time per game tick, meaning there are some rare situations (such as not playing the game for more than a year) where you may not get all of the time you were away" and warns that stretched ticks make autobuyers fire less often — [AD h2p.js "Offline Progress"](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/secret-formula/h2p.js).
- The simulation shows a progress-bar modal ("Offline Progress Simulation") with "Speed up" (halve remaining ticks, min 500) and "SKIP" (finish in 10 ticks) buttons — [AD game.js L995-1012](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/game.js). After more than 14 days away, a separate catch-up modal is shown and "painful" notations/themes are reset — [storage.js L525-531](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/storage.js).
- The live game loop clamps each update's elapsed time to 1 day (`Math.clamp(thisUpdate - player.lastUpdate, 1, 8.64e7)`) and applies missed time as offline progress after >60 s unfocused (`hibernationCatchup`) — [game.js L439-451](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/game.js).

**Kittens Game (primary source)**
- "Redshift" offline catch-up: `daysOffset = Math.round(delta / 2000)` (one game day per 2 real seconds), ignored under 3 days; capped at 10 in-game years, or 40 once year ≥1000 or paragon > 0 (`maxYears` × `daysPerSeason` × `seasonsPerYear`); redshift is always on for mobile and an option on web — [kittensgame js/time.js L233-258](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/master/js/time.js). `applyRedshift` fast-forwards resources, calendar, buildings, workshop, village, space and religion and then re-enforces storage limits — [time.js L191-232](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/master/js/time.js).
- A second, non-punishing mechanic: with Calendar researched the game also banks "temporal flux" at 5 per 60 s away, later spent on accelerated time — [time.js L106-131](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/master/js/time.js).

**Evolve (primary source)**
- No offline resource gain; instead after ≥120 s away the game banks "accelerated time": `at += floor(2/3 * timeDiff * timeAccelerationFactor / gameDayDuration)`, "capped at 8*60*60/2.5 game days" (11,520), and any stored value above the cap is discarded — [Evolve functions.js L176-205](https://github.com/pmotschmann/Evolve/blob/master/src/functions.js). The live loop's catch-up is limited to "1 minute (12 game days) of simulation per call" with the comment "Currently there is no smart catch-up mechanism" — [Evolve main.js L842-845](https://github.com/pmotschmann/Evolve/blob/master/src/main.js).

**Other games (snippets)**
- IdleOn: a "24 HR Time Candy" instantly grants 24 hours of AFK gains and is crafted from many boss/monster materials — [IdleOn 24 HR Time Candy wiki](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/24_HR_Time_Candy) (search snippet; page blocked); players report it is "generally more efficient to AFK for around 6 hours or so ... based on your inventory capacity" — [Steam IdleOn discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/3/3836550304879109385/) (search snippet; page blocked).
- NGU: progress-bar content runs at 100% speed offline; Adventure item drops are online-only — [NGU on namu.wiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/NGU%20Idle) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Idle Slayer: the Idle Stone's offline slay speed is capped at 90% (level 32) — [Idle Slayer Stones of Time wiki](https://idleslayer.fandom.com/wiki/Stones_of_Time) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Retention rationale in commentary: search summaries of the tideward.app comparison and design blogs argued that unlimited offline time "train[s] players to never open the app daily" and that "twenty-four hours encourages a daily check-in without punishing missed days", while critics note Melvor "players are required to interact with the game every single day in order to maintain character progression" — [tideward.app](https://tideward.app/offline-progression/), [Melvor Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/discussions/0/3192493998739513526/) (search snippets; attribution between these two pages is uncertain because both were blocked).

### Inferences
- (Inference) The 24-hour, 100%-rate simulation is the de-facto standard for RuneScape-like skilling idles (Melvor, Trimps' Time Warp); reduced-efficiency offline (Cookie Clicker's 5%→91%) belongs to prestige-driven clickers where offline is itself an unlockable reward. For Fantasy Idle (Melvor-like, no prestige), a hard cap of 12–24 h at 100% efficiency matches player expectations; if a "softer" cap is wanted, use Cookie Clicker's structure (full rate up to T, then 10% of rate beyond T) rather than a hard stop.
- (Inference) Fantasy Idle's current "uncapped, gathering-only" offline is the worst of both worlds: uncapped rewards long absences (Melvor moved from 12→18→24 h under player pressure, but never uncapped), while ignoring combat/crafting punishes the most common idle activity. Simulate whatever the active action is (including combat with food consumption, stop-on-death, and stop-when-materials-run-out, as Melvor does) using the *same* tick code as online play — Melvor's #1126 (offline mining at 38%) and #4740 (background-tick loss) show what goes wrong when offline uses a different path or relies on background ticks instead of timestamps.
- (Inference) Antimatter Dimensions' rule "no tick shorter than 33 ms, no tick longer than 1 day, at most 1e5 ticks" is a good template for a bounded replay: for a 24 h cap and 100 ms ticks that is 864,000 ticks (Trimps' exact number); batching by action duration (compute whole actions, not frames) keeps this cheap.
- (Inference) Show one consolidated "welcome back" summary (Melvor users complain when it repeats or cannot be dismissed — see Key question 7) and include per-hour rates (food/hour as Melvor does) so players can plan the next absence.

### Gaps
- Melvor's exact offline cap history (12→18→24 h) and the developer's stated reason were not retrievable from a primary source; the 18 h stage is only evidenced by issue #1703 and a Steam thread title.
- IdleOn's underlying AFK time cap (if any) was not confirmed; the sources only describe candy and inventory limits.
- Cookie Clicker's and Melvor's mobile-specific offline differences beyond the `Game.mobile` flag were not investigated.

## Key question 3: How do idle games pace onboarding and unlocks (progressive disclosure, first hook, tutorial patterns), and what fails?

### Takeaway
The successful pattern is state-driven progressive disclosure: tabs/buttons stay hidden or dimmed until a condition is met (Kittens Game hides every tab but Bonfire; Antimatter Dimensions highlights the next button through six states), tutorials advance on what the player has *done* rather than on clicking "Next" (Trimps' 15-step advisor window), and the first goal is minutes away (AD's second dimension at 100 antimatter; Melvor's tutorial needs 83 XP). Forced modal steps and long text are absent from all the reachable primary sources; RuneScape's forced random events are the documented failure of interruptive design.

### Cited Findings
- Progressive disclosure is "an interaction design pattern ... deferring some advanced or rarely-used features to a secondary screen ... where information is revealed when it becomes relevant to the current task" — [Wikipedia, Progressive disclosure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_disclosure) (search snippet; page blocked).

### Inferences
- (Inference) Every reachable implementation gates by *state predicates* evaluated each tick (Kittens `visible = condition`, AD `condition: () => ...`, Trimps `if (...) tutorialStep++`), never by a linear "must click here next" script. Fantasy Idle's linear tab-unlock tutorial is being bypassed precisely because it is order-based; replacing it with per-tab unlock predicates (e.g., Combat tab visible at Woodcutting 5 or 100 gold; Crafting visible once 10 logs are held) removes the need for a bypass.
- (Inference) First-hook timing benchmarks: AD's first new button at 100 antimatter (well under a minute), Melvor's tutorial completion at 83 XP (a handful of actions), first prestige in AD "a few hours to a couple of days". A reasonable target for Fantasy Idle: first new tab within 2–3 minutes, first equipment upgrade within 10 minutes, all core tabs revealed within the first hour, first long-horizon system (mastery/expansion) at 1–3 hours.
- (Inference) Use Trimps' re-openable, flashing "advisor" panel and AD's highlighted-next-button rather than modals: both are non-blocking, and both hide entirely after the player proves competence (Trimps skips veterans to the last step; AD disables after game completion).

## Key question 4: How do achievements grant real bonuses, and how large are typical per-achievement effects?

### Takeaway
Games that make achievements matter use one of two shapes: a *count-based global multiplier* (Cookie Clicker: 4% milk per achievement feeding 17 kitten upgrades; Antimatter Dimensions: ×1.03 per achievement and ×1.25 per completed row; Evolve: +0.25% per achievement level; Trimps: a summed percentage applied as `1 + bonus/100`) or *specific per-achievement rewards* (AD's individual rewards are mostly +2% to +50% to one dimension, some QoL). Melvor deliberately keeps completion cosmetic until 100%, when the Cape of Completion grants 5% damage reduction and +25% mastery pool cap.

### Cited Findings
- Cookie Clicker: `Game.milkProgress=Game.AchievementsOwned/25;` (4% per achievement) and only pool `''`/`'normal'` achievements count (`CountsAsAchievementOwned`), excluding shadow achievements — [ozh/cookieclicker main.js L5240, L12963-12966](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). Kitten upgrades multiply CpS by `(1+Game.milkProgress*factor*milkMult)` with factors 0.1 (helpers), 0.125, 0.15, 0.175, 0.2 (managers, accountants, specialists, experts, consultants), 0.175, 0.15, 0.125, 0.115, 0.11, 0.105, 0.1 (angels) and 0.05 (Fortune #103) — [main.js L5256-5272](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). The wiki counts 622 milk-eligible achievements (2,488% milk) and states the full kitten stack multiplies CpS by ~1.18×10^14 — [Cookie Clicker Milk wiki](https://cookieclicker.wiki.gg/wiki/Milk) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Antimatter Dimensions: global achievement power `basePower = Math.pow(1.25, unlockedRows) * Math.pow(1.03, Achievements.effectiveCount)` (raised to a glyph/Ra exponent) — [AD normal-achievement.js L167-172](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/achievements/normal-achievement.js). Individual rewards include "Start with 100 antimatter", "8th Antimatter Dimensions are 10% stronger", "1st Antimatter Dimensions are 5% stronger", "Dimensions 1-7 are 2% stronger", "Multiply starting tick speed by 1.02", "Antimatter Dimensions 1-4 are 25% stronger", "1st Antimatter Dimensions are 3 times stronger", "Dimension Autobuyer bulks are unlimited", and time-based multipliers — [AD normal-achievements.js](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/secret-formula/achievements/normal-achievements.js).
- Evolve: "mastery" from achievements is `aLvl * m_rate` where each achievement contributes up to 5 levels and `m_rate` is 0.25% per level in the standard universe (0.15% elsewhere) plus a universe-specific 0.10–0.15% per level; it requires the "challenge" gene ≥ 2 — [Evolve achieve.js `universeLevel`](https://github.com/pmotschmann/Evolve/blob/master/src/achieve.js), [functions.js `masteryType`](https://github.com/pmotschmann/Evolve/blob/master/src/functions.js).
- Trimps: achievements are tiered breakpoint lists (e.g., Zone Progress breakpoints 2, 5, 10, 20 … with tiers 1–12) — [Trimps config.js L6961-6980](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/config.js); the aggregate is applied as `achievementBonus: () => 1 + game.global.achievementBonus / 100` — [Trimps main.js L12250](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/main.js); in v5.8.0 "Achievement percentage above 50k now grants 1 GU every 10k%" — [updates.html](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/updates.html).
- Idle Slayer: "upgrades that increase your Bonus CpS by a certain percentage based on Achievements unlocked. These upgrades are additive with each other and increase your Bonus CpS multiplicatively"; the game has 650+ achievements — [Idle Slayer Upgrades wiki](https://idleslayer.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrades), [Steam achievements guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2981185240) (search snippets; pages blocked).
- Melvor: the Cape of Completion "can only be purchased once the player has 100% completion", combines all skillcape effects, and gives "5% Damage Reduction and +25% Mastery Pool Cap" only when worn; 100% requires max levels, level-99 mastery on every action, every item found, every monster killed, every pet — [Melvor wiki Cape of Completion](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Cape_of_Completion), [Completion Log](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Completion_Log) (search snippets; pages blocked). Melvor's per-skill mastery instead grants checkpoint bonuses at 10/25/50/95% of the mastery pool (e.g., Woodcutting +5% double-log chance per 10 levels) — [Melvor wiki Mastery](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Mastery) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Kittens Game: the Achievements tab only appears once an achievement is unlocked — [kittensgame game.js L3178](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/master/game.js).

### Inferences
- (Inference) Typical per-achievement magnitudes in the genre are small and compounding: 3% (AD), 4% of a secondary stat (Cookie Clicker milk), 0.25% (Evolve), with occasional row/tier jumps (AD ×1.25 per row). For Fantasy Idle, the un-applied "+10% Max HP" style rewards are on the generous side but fine as *specific* rewards if they are few; a safer pattern is a global "+1% skill speed & +1% damage per achievement" (or per achievement tier) that is cheap to implement in one place and cannot be forgotten, plus a handful of named milestone rewards.
- (Inference) Permanent multipliers (not cosmetic) are the norm in every reachable game except Melvor, and even Melvor attaches a concrete reward to completion; cosmetic-only achievements are not supported by the sources as a retention tool.
- (Inference) Any achievement bonus must be applied through the same modifier pipeline as equipment/skills so it shows up in the displayed rates; Fantasy Idle's current bug (rewards listed but never applied) is exactly the kind of defect a single `achievementBonus()` accessor like Trimps' prevents.

- Idle Slayer's exact per-achievement percentage and the names of the achievement-based upgrades were not retrievable.
- NGU Idle's achievement effects were not researched (no reachable source).

## Key question 5: What daily/weekly retention loops do idle games use, which are healthy for a hobby project, and how are rewards scaled?

### Takeaway
Reachable idle games favour *bankable, non-punishing* daily loops: NGU's Daily Spin banks 36 hours by default and up to 7 days with an upgrade; Cookie Clicker's sugar lump ripens on a ~24 h cycle and auto-harvests (with offline lumps credited on load) so missing a day costs nothing; IdleOn gates its mini-games by daily play counts and real-time timers. Streak-based login rewards are documented as a dark pattern that converts a reward into an obligation, especially when paired with paid streak restores.

### Cited Findings
- NGU Idle Daily Spin: one spin per 24 h; "without this upgrade, the game will bank 36 hours for you"; a 100,000 AP upgrade allows stacking up to 7 days; the wheel upgrades to better tiers at cumulative 7, 14, 30, 60, 120, 180 and 365 spins — [NGU Idle Daily Spin wiki](https://ngu-idle.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Spin), [NGU Idle guide](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1uRqbSjnqqZMXhfpM4A1SCioXpB6Ng6SCAEWKbstVP5E/mobilebasic) (search snippets; pages blocked).
- Cookie Clicker sugar lumps: `lumpMatureAge=hour*20; lumpRipeAge=hour*23; lumpOverripeAge=lumpRipeAge+hour` (24 h), the tooltip says "If you do nothing, it will auto-harvest", and on load `Game.loadLumps` credits `Math.floor(age/Game.lumpOverripeAge)` lumps harvested while away — [ozh/cookieclicker main.js L4613, L4634-4669](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js).
- IdleOn: +4 daily minigame plays per Gem Shop purchase, not stacking day to day; Spike minigame's first daily play awards up to 30 gems; Darts uses a real-time reset timer; a "Daily Checklist" page exists on the wiki — [DigitalTQ](https://www.digitaltq.com/wiki/idleon/items/misc/daily-minigame-plays-1953), [IdleOn Minigames wiki](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Minigames), [IdleOn Daily Checklist](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Daily_Checklist) (search snippets; pages blocked).
- Kittens Game banks 5 "temporal flux" per minute away (with Calendar) as a future speed-up rather than a login reward — [kittensgame time.js L106-131](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/master/js/time.js).
- Dark-pattern definition: daily rewards "reward you the first time you play the game each day"; with streaks "if you miss a day, you 'break the chain' and lose your bonus", and some games sell streak restores, so "games can get people to pay to avoid punishment" — [DarkPattern.games, Daily Rewards](https://www.darkpattern.games/pattern/11/daily-rewards.html) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Behavioural analysis: streak systems rely on "habit loops, loss aversion, and obligation-based engagement"; "the true reward isn't the tangible value itself, but continuity" — [Medium/Bootcamp, streaks and daily rewards](https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/streaks-and-daily-rewards-as-habit-forming-systems-dab7f5a34539) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Prevalence: a 2025 study of Korean mobile games found daily login rewards "used by 95% of games" — [arXiv 2504.10714](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.10714) (search snippet; page blocked). Path of Exile's Chris Wilson publicly criticised such live-service "design tricks" — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/when-i-was-growing-up-games-were-designed-to-be-fun-not-manipulative-path-of-exile-co-creator-shines-a-blacklight-on-the-bulls-t-design-tricks-infesting-all-my-live-service-games/) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Melvor's welcome-back flow shows the offline summary as the return ritual; no login-reward or streak system appears in any reachable Melvor source (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- (Inference) The healthy shape for a hobby project is "bankable claimable" rather than "streak": a reward that accrues on a ~24 h timer, can be held for several days (NGU banks 36 h–7 d), and is auto-collected or never lost (Cookie Clicker lumps). Concretely for Fantasy Idle: replace the login-streak reward with a "daily cache" that ripens every 20–24 h, stores up to 3, and is claimed from the UI; never reset anything for missing a day; no paid restores.
- (Inference) Scale the daily reward with progress by tying it to the player's current rates rather than fixed amounts — e.g., Cookie Clicker's Lucky formula (`min(15% of bank, 15 min of production)`) is a ready-made, progress-scaled amount; a daily cache worth 30–60 minutes of the player's current gold/XP rate stays relevant from level 1 to endgame.
- (Inference) Daily *tasks* (IdleOn-style "do 20 fishing actions") are acceptable if they are optional and rotate; avoid weekly structures that require ≥7 consecutive days.

### Gaps
- Melvor's explicit stance on dailies (the brief says "Melvor doesn't") could not be sourced: the wiki and Steam were blocked and the interviews reachable via snippets do not mention it.
- IdleOn's daily/weekly quest lists and AFK Arena/Idle Heroes daily loop details were not retrievable (wiki/Steam blocked; no design deconstruction page was reachable).
- No quantitative evidence on reward scaling formulas for daily rewards was found in any reachable source.

## Key question 6: What save formats, versioning/migration schemes, cloud-sync conflict rules and cheat-resistance approaches do browser idle games use?

### Takeaway
Every reachable game stores JSON in localStorage and exports a compressed, Base64-ish string with an explicit version: Cookie Clicker (pipe-delimited, Base64 + `!END!`, refuses future versions, inline `if (version<…)` migrations), Antimatter Dimensions (pako deflate → Base64 with character substitutions and a magic prefix, a numeric migration chain 1…25, 3 slots × 8 timed backups, SHA-512/256-hashed cloud saves, conflict modal comparing progress *and* time), Trimps/Evolve/Kittens (LZString). Melvor's cloud (PlayFab-backed) does not validate saves and its reload-to-reroll exploits were closed as "notabug"; server-side validation is only used by games with leaderboards/economies.

### Cited Findings
**Cookie Clicker (primary source)**
- Save string = pipe-separated sections, version first: `version=parseFloat(str[0])`; import fails if `isNaN(version) || str.length<5`; loading a save from a newer version is refused with "You are attempting to load a save from a future version" — [ozh/cookieclicker main.js L3160-3177](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). Export is `utf8_to_b64(str)+'!END!'` (L3049-3086) and import strips at `'!END!'` (L3154). Migrations are inline version checks such as `if (version<1.0503)//upgrades that used to be regular, but are now heavenly` and `if (version<1.035)//old non-binary algorithm` (L3306, L3341, L3458).
- Autosave every 60 s: `Game.T%(Game.fps*60)==0 ... && Game.prefs.autosave` plus ctrl+S ("the game autosaves every 60 seconds") — [main.js L6893, L17017](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js). Wiki: the save is Base64 "appended with !END! ... to ensure portability", documented per-field as of v2.052 — [Cookie Clicker Save wiki](https://cookieclicker.wiki.gg/wiki/Save) (search snippet; page blocked).

**Antimatter Dimensions (primary source)**
- Serializer: JSON with `Infinity`→`"Infinity"` and Sets→arrays → pako deflate → byte string → `btoa` → substitutions (`0`→`0a`, `+`→`0b`, `/`→`0c`, trailing `=` removed "to prevent accidental truncation when double-clicking") → prefix `AntimatterDimensionsSavefileFormat` + version code (e.g., `AAB`) and suffix `EndOfSavefile`; unrecognised strings fall back to legacy `atob` for pre-Reality saves starting `eYJ` — [AD serializer.js](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/serializer.js).
- Migrations: `patches` keyed by numeric version (1 … 25, fractional keys like 12.1 allowed), applied in sorted order `while ((version = versions.find(v => player.version < v && v < maxVersion)) !== undefined)`; a separate pre-Reality chain up to version 13 — [AD migrations.js](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/migrations.js). Loading dispatches `SAVE_CONVERTED_FROM_PREVIOUS_VERSION` for pre-Reality saves and shows "Could not load the save (format unrecognized or invalid)" on failure — [AD storage.js L160, L446-450](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/storage.js).
- Storage: localStorage key `dimensionSave`, 3 save slots, default autosave interval 30,000 ms, and 8 automatic backup slots — online backups every 1 min, 5 min, 20 min, 1 h; offline backups at 10 min, 1 h, 5 h of absence; one reserve slot — [AD storage.js L17-60, L91-97](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/storage.js), [player.js L806](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/player.js).
- Cloud conflicts: `ProgressChecker.compareSaveProgress` maps each save to a composite progress stage and treats a difference > 0.05 as "farther"; `compareSaveTimes` compares `records.realTimePlayed`; on load a modal always appears when there is a conflict (older local save, farther cloud progress, or different names); on save the modal appears when `forceModal || (hasConflict && player.options.showCloudModal)`, and `forceCloudOverwrite` skips it; cloud data is hashed with `sha512_256(GameSaveSerializer.serialize(cloudSave))` — [AD cloud-saving.js](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/cloud-saving.js), [progress-checker.js](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/progress-checker.js). Defaults: `cloudEnabled: true, showCloudModal: true, forceCloudOverwrite: false` — [player.js L784-787](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/player.js).

**Trimps, Kittens, Evolve (primary source)**
- Trimps: `saveString = LZString.compressToBase64(JSON.stringify(saveGame))` into `localStorage["trimpSave1"]`, with `trimpBackup1`; autosave via `setTimeout(autoSave, 60000)`; version compared on load (`oldVersion = savegame.global.version`) with a reset message for incompatible 0.07 saves; the export text advises using Trimps' own LZString copy "to ensure there are no differences between our versions" — [Trimps main.js L37-46, L219-327, L816, L3653](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io/blob/master/main.js).
- Kittens: `saveVersion: 15`; raw JSON in localStorage until it outgrows "the 5mb localStorage limit", then an LZ blob (`compressToUTF16`); import accepts Base64 or UTF-16 LZ; quota-exceeded/private-browsing failure is handled — [kittensgame game.js L2345, L2873-3040](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/master/game.js).
- Evolve: `save.setItem('evolved', LZString.compressToUTF16(JSON.stringify(global)))` each long loop; export is `LZString.compressToBase64`; import runs legacy fix-ups (e.g., pre-v1.1.4 message colours) — [Evolve main.js L12824](https://github.com/pmotschmann/Evolve/blob/master/src/main.js), [functions.js `exportGame`/`importGame`](https://github.com/pmotschmann/Evolve/blob/master/src/functions.js).

**Melvor Idle**
- Save format: "Previously, the save string was a JSON object compressed with gzip and then encoded in base64. - Firestone (online idle RPG) runs "automated scripts that ban cheaters based on abnormal values in their save files and leaderboard scores, with the script running every few minutes" — [Firestone Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1013320/discussions/0/3159763879073035335/) (search snippet; page blocked).
- Save editors exist for Cookie Clicker, Antimatter Dimensions and Reactor Idle, confirming that client-side saves are freely editable — [AD Save Editor](https://github.com/Sato-Isolated/Antimatter-Dimensions-Save-Editor), [Reactor Idle editor](https://quedlin.github.io/reactor-idle-savegame-editor/), [Cookie Clicker Cheating wiki](https://cookieclicker.fandom.com/wiki/Cheating).
- Steam Cloud's generic conflict UI shows local and cloud modification timestamps and lets the user pick; guides warn not to "automatically choose the cloud copy" — [SteamVaults](https://steamvaults.org/blog/steam-cloud-out-of-sync-save-conflict), [savegamelocation.com](https://savegamelocation.com/blog/steam-cloud-not-syncing-fix-save-conflicts/) (search snippets; pages blocked).

### Inferences
- (Inference) For Fantasy Idle's JSON-every-10-s localStorage save: keep JSON (every reachable game does), add a top-level integer `saveVersion` and an AD-style ordered migration map (`{2: s => ..., 3: s => ...}` applied while `save.version < key`), refuse saves from a newer version with a clear message (Cookie Clicker), compress exports with LZString/pako + Base64 and wrap them in a magic prefix/suffix so truncated pastes are detected (AD), and keep timed rolling backups (AD's 1 m/5 m/20 m/1 h online and 10 m/1 h/5 h offline slots are a proven schedule).
- (Inference) For the unused Express/Postgres cloud API: store the blob plus `{version, savedAt (client), receivedAt (server), playtimeSeconds, progressScore}`; on load, if the cloud copy is *both* newer and farther (AD's two-signal check), load it silently; if the signals disagree, show a choose-modal with both timestamps and a one-line progress summary (Steam/AD pattern); never auto-overwrite cloud from a device that has not loaded the latest cloud save (Melvor #4094 root cause). Last-write-wins alone is what lost Melvor players months of progress.
- (Inference) Cheat resistance that is *feasible* without leaderboards: (1) roll RNG outcomes at drop time (pre-rolled chests, as suggested in Melvor #2328) so reload-rerolls are pointless; (2) server-computed offline gains from server timestamps (the server knows `receivedAt` of the last save, so offline duration cannot be forged by changing the device clock); (3) plausibility checks on upload (XP/gold delta vs elapsed time × max known rate) that flag rather than reject; (4) an HMAC over the blob if you want to detect casual localStorage edits, accepting that a browser game can never hide the key. Fully server-authoritative simulation or replay validation is only justified if leaderboards/trading are added.
- (Inference) Save every 10 s is fine, but also save on `visibilitychange`/`pagehide` and immediately before any irreversible RNG (chest opening) — the Melvor exploit reports all hinge on the window between an RNG event and the next save.

## Key question 7: What UI conventions (layout, number formatting, progress bars, notifications, mobile, accessibility) do idle games use, and what do players praise or criticise in Melvor's UI?

### Takeaway
Layout convention (Melvor): a persistent left sidebar listing skills with levels and a top bar with HP/gold, praised for clarity but criticised as cluttered and "boring". Number formatting: full digits with thousands separators below 1,000,000, then K/M/B (Kittens starts suffixes at 9,000; Cookie Clicker at 1,000,000), with scientific/engineering as a user option and as the fallback once letter suffixes run out; every large open-source idle exposes the notation as a setting. Offline-return summaries must be a single dismissible modal (Melvor players complain about repeats and the inability to disable it); reduced-motion should be honoured via `prefers-reduced-motion` plus explicit toggles for particles/animations.

**Number formatting (primary + secondary)**
- Cookie Clicker: numbers below 1,000,000 print in full with comma separators; from 1,000,000 the short suffix table `['k','M','B','T','Qa','Qi','Sx','Sp','Oc','No', 'Dc', …]` (or long names " million", " billion", …) applies via `formatEveryThirdPower`, which only engages when `val>=1000000`; the "raw" preference switches to `toPrecision(3)` scientific for huge values; values are rounded to 3 decimals — [ozh/cookieclicker main.js L217-262, L264-290](https://github.com/ozh/cookieclicker/blob/master/main.js).
- Kittens Game: SI-style suffixes with thresholds `K` at ≥9e3, `M` 1e6, `G` 1e9, `T` 1e12, `P` 1e15, `E`, `Z`, `Y`, then invented `U/S/H/F/L/W/Q`; notation options `si`, `e` (scientific from 1e4) and `sie` (SI below 1e15, then scientific) — [kittensgame game.js L5019-5035 and `getDisplayValueExt`](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/master/game.js).
- Antimatter Dimensions: default notation "Mixed scientific"; 22 selectable notations, with Standard (K/M/B names), Emoji, Roman, etc. flagged `isPainful` and automatically replaced by Mixed scientific after a 2-week absence — [AD notations.js](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/notations.js), [player.js L775](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/player.js), [storage.js L527-529](https://github.com/IvarK/AntimatterDimensionsSourceCode/blob/master/src/core/storage/storage.js).
- Industry write-up: letter notation (K, M, B, T, … AA, AB) reaches its limit at 999.99 Z then switches to scientific; an extended ZZ scheme reaches 2,043 digits; engineering notation keeps exponents divisible by 3 so "e3/e6/e9" map to thousand/million/billion; scientific makes comparison easier because only the exponent must be compared — [InnoGames blog, Dealing with huge numbers in idle games](https://blog.innogames.com/dealing-with-huge-numbers-in-idle-games/), [Steam guide Number Notation](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2773222340) (search snippets; pages blocked).

**Accessibility / reduced motion**

- Trimps ships a separate `ScreenReader.html` entry point — [Trimps repository root](https://github.com/Trimps/Trimps.github.io).

### Inferences
- (Inference) Formatting thresholds for Fantasy Idle: show integers with thousands separators up to 999,999, switch to one-decimal `K/M/B/T` from 1,000,000 (Cookie Clicker) or from 10,000 if the HUD is narrow (Kittens' 9,000 threshold exists for the same reason), keep 3 significant digits, and offer a "scientific" toggle; XP-to-next-level and action timers should show as raw numbers/seconds, not suffixed.
- (Inference) The genre's mobile answer is a collapsible sidebar (Melvor) rather than bottom tabs; the recurring complaint is density, so hide locked skills/tabs (Kittens) and collapse secondary panels by default.
- (Inference) Offline return: one modal, dismissible, with a "don't show for <1 min absences" threshold (AD ignores <10 s and uses a fast path <50 s) and modal queuing so toasts never replace it (Melvor #4229).
- (Inference) Implement reduced motion three ways at once: honour `prefers-reduced-motion` in CSS, expose "reduce animations/particles" toggles (Cookie Clicker/AD), and make the combat/mini-game feedback readable without motion (text/colour state), since the mini-games are timing-based.
