# Combat scaling, combat rewards and prestige loops in idle games (research notes for the Fantasy Idle redesign)

Scope note on sourcing: in this session the fandom wikis (Clicker Heroes, Idle Slayer, TT2, NGU, Realm Grinder, AD), wiki.melvoridle.com, gamedeveloper.com, GDC Vault, archive.org, Steam and Reddit were all blocked by the network egress proxy, and the web-search budget ran out part-way through. Findings therefore come from three tiers of sources, marked in each bullet:
- **[source code]** – game or simulator code fetched from GitHub (the de-obfuscated Melvor Idle source dump, the Clicker Heroes Tracker/Calculator models, the Antimatter Dimensions source, the TT2-Sim server variables). These are the most reliable formulas here.
- **[article mirror]** – Anthony Pecorella's three-part "Math of Idle Games" (Kongregate/GameAnalytics), read in full from a GitHub mirror of the GameAnalytics reprint.
- **[search summary]** – facts that were only visible in search-engine summaries of blocked wiki/blog/forum pages. The page URL is cited so the report writer can verify, but the page text itself was not read. Treat numbers from these as "probable, unverified".

## KQ1. Clicker Heroes: monster HP regimes, gold per kill, hero souls, ascension, hero cost vs HP growth, primal bosses, transcendence

### Takeaway
Clicker Heroes pairs an exponential enemy curve (×1.55/zone to zone 140, then ×1.145/zone, creeping up by +0.001 every 500 zones to a 1.545 cap) with gold that is literally a fraction of monster HP (HP/15, times up to 3× past zone 75), hero costs that grow ×1.07 per level, and a prestige currency (Hero Souls, +10% DPS each) whose post-transcendence reward is exponential in the zone reached (20·(1+TP)^(zone/5−20) per primal boss); a second prestige layer (Ancient Souls = 5·log10(total souls)) feeds Transcendent Power, which is the exponent base that keeps souls growing as fast as monsters.

### Cited Findings

**Monster HP (current formula, "monsterLifeFormula1")** – [source code] Clicker Heroes Tracker `monster.ts` (a port of the game's ActionScript; constants: `earlyBase = 1.55`, `lateBase = 1.145`, `levelWhereScaleChanges = 140`, `increasePerFiveHundredLevels = 0.001`, `healthAt200k = 1.240e25409`, `BOSS_ZONE_MODULUS = 5`):
- zone ≤ 140: `HP = ceil(1.55^(zone−1)·10 + (zone−1)·10)`; boss zones (zone % 5 == 0) multiply by `getBossHpMultiplier(zone)` before the ceil.
- 140 < zone ≤ 500: `HP = 1.145^(zone−140) · HP(140) + (140−1)·10`.
- 500 < zone ≤ 200,000: base = `1.145 + floor(zone/500)·0.001`, `HP = base^(zone mod 500) · HP(previous 500 mark) + 1390`, where each 500-block is `HP(k·500) = (1.145 + (k−1)·0.001)^500 · HP((k−1)·500)`.
- zone > 200,000: `HP = 1.240e25409 · 1.545^(zone−200001)`.
- Legacy saves (`!transcendent && highestFinishedZonePersist > 500`) use the old formula `oldMonsterLifeFormula1`: `HP = ceil((1.6^(min(zone,140)−1) + (min(zone,140)−1)·10) · 1.15^(max(zone−140,0)))` with the same boss multiplier — i.e. the "1.6x / 1.15x" regime the wiki describes is the pre-transcendence formula and "1.55x / 1.145x" is the current one. — [dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker monster.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/models/monster.ts)
- The 2015 VikeStep calculator encodes the old formula directly: `monsterLife = ceil((10·(1.6^(min(140,level)−1) + min(140,level) − 1)) · 1.15^(max(140,level)−140))`, and boss HP as that ×10 × (1 − 0.02·Bubos level). — [VikeStep/Clicker-Heroes-Calculator ClickerHeroesCalculator.js](https://github.com/VikeStep/Clicker-Heroes-Calculator/blob/326dd2e59c0df12bed6de05310d2118730aeeeba/ClickerHeroesCalculator.js)
- [search summary] The wiki's Monsters/Zones pages state the same regimes in prose: 1.55× per zone to 140, 1.145× per zone to 500, then (post-transcendence) 1.146 to zone 1000, 1.147 to 1500 … up to 1.544 until zone 200,000 and 1.545 after; level-1 monsters have 10 HP. — [Clicker Heroes wiki: Monsters](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Monsters), [Zones](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Zones)

**Boss HP multiplier and treasure chests** – [source code] `userData.ts`: `BOSS_HP_MULTIPLIER = 10`, `MINIMUM_BOSS_HP_MULTIPLIER = 5`, `BOSS_HEALTH_INCREASE_PER_ZONE_INTERVAL = 0.04` per `BOSS_HEALTH_INCREASE_ZONE_INTERVAL = 500` zones; `getBossHpMultiplier(level) = max(uncapped − Bubos reduction, 5)`. Treasure chests: `TREASURE_MULTIPLIER = 10` (gold), `CHANCE_OF_TREASURE_MONSTER = 0.01`, decreasing every 500 zones to `MINIMUM_TREASURE_CHANCE_MULTIPLIER = 0.01`. Hero-soul DPS bonus: `getHeroSoulWorldDamageBonus() = heroSouls·10 + ancients.dpsPercent` (percent), i.e. +10% DPS per soul plus Morgulis. — [userData.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/models/userData.ts)
- VikeStep's calculator: `heroSoulsMultiplier = 1 + heroSouls/10 + Morgulis_level·0.11`. — [ClickerHeroesCalculator.js](https://github.com/VikeStep/Clicker-Heroes-Calculator/blob/326dd2e59c0df12bed6de05310d2118730aeeeba/ClickerHeroesCalculator.js)

**Gold per kill** – [source code] `monsterGoldFormula1()`: `gold = ceil( oldMonsterLifeFormula1() / 15 · goldMultiplier · chestMultiplier · (1 + ancientGoldPercent/100) · zoneBonus )` with `zoneBonus = min(3, 1.025^(zone−75))` for zone > 75 (else 1). Note the gold formula divides the *old* (1.6/1.15) HP formula, not the current one. — [monster.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/models/monster.ts)
- VikeStep (2015): `monsterGold = ceil((monsterLife/15) · goldMultiplier · min(3, 1.025^max(0, level−75)) · (1 + 0.05·Mammon_level))`, plus `(1 + 0.225·Mimzee_level)` for chests. — [ClickerHeroesCalculator.js](https://github.com/VikeStep/Clicker-Heroes-Calculator/blob/326dd2e59c0df12bed6de05310d2118730aeeeba/ClickerHeroesCalculator.js)
- [search summary] Wiki Gold page: "as the monsters get more HP, their Gold drop will also increase to keep up with the increased cost of the heroes that need to kill the monsters." — [Clicker Heroes wiki: Gold](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Gold)

**Monsters per zone, boss timer** – [search summary] Regular zones require killing 10 (+0.1 per 500 zones) monsters; boss zones require one. Kumawakamaru reduces the count (effect −8·(1−e^(−0.025·level)) per the Tracker), minimum 2 monsters. Bosses must be killed within 30 s; Chronos adds up to +30 s with diminishing returns per level. — [Clicker Heroes wiki: Kumawakamaru](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Kumawakamaru,_Ancient_of_Shadows) (search summary); Kuma/Atman/Bubos effect curves `kuma = −8·(1−e^(−0.025L))`, `atman = 75·(1−e^(−0.013L))`, `bubos = −5·(1−e^(−0.002L))` from [outsiderSuggestions.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/components/outsiderSuggestions/outsiderSuggestions.ts) [source code].

**Hero level cost and DPS** – [source code] VikeStep: `cost(level→level+1) = floor(baseCost · 1.07^level · (1 − 0.02·Dogcog_level))` (special cases `(5+level)·1.07^level` for Cid and `20·1.07^level`); hero DPS = `level · baseDPS · achievementMult · darkRitualMult · allHeroMult · heroSoulsMult · levelMultiplier · gild bonus`, with the ×4 "DPS4x" and ×10 milestone multipliers (`Math.pow(4, 32)` at the 1000-level cap). — [ClickerHeroesCalculator.js](https://github.com/VikeStep/Clicker-Heroes-Calculator/blob/326dd2e59c0df12bed6de05310d2118730aeeeba/ClickerHeroesCalculator.js)
- [source code] Tracker: `heroCostMultiplier = 1.07` (1.22 beyond zone 1.2e6, used by the Ace Scout heroes) and `heroDamageMultiplier = 4` per 25-level milestone (4.5 beyond zone 168k, 1000 beyond 1.2e6). — [outsiderSuggestions.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/components/outsiderSuggestions/outsiderSuggestions.ts)
- [search summary] Wiki Heroes page: `Floor(Floor(BaseCost × 1.07^CurrentLevel) × CostReduction)`, e.g. Treebeast 114→115 costs `50 × 1.07^114 = 111,871`; Ace Scouts use 1.22; "each level is 1.2% less efficient of a DPS increase vs the cost (≈0.988 per level)". — [Clicker Heroes wiki: Heroes](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Heroes)

**Hero souls (prestige currency) per ascension** – [search summary, official blog] Primal-boss souls before transcending: `Ordinary_HS = ((zone − 80)/25)^1.3 · (1 + PonyboyBonus)`; after transcending: `Transcendent_HS = 20 · (1 + TranscendentPower)^(zone/5 − 20) · (1 + PonyboyBonus)`. Primal bosses start at zone 100 with a 25% chance per boss zone; zones 110/120/130 are always primal; the chance drops 2% every 500 zones to a 5% floor. Ascension also grants 1 HS per 2,000 hero levels bought. — [Clicker Heroes blog: Primal Bosses](https://blog.clickerheroes.com/primal-bosses-clicker-heroes-tips-and-farming/), [blog: How to get hero souls](https://blog.clickerheroes.com/how-to-get-hero-souls-in-clicker-heroes-complete-guide/) (search summaries)
- [source code] The Tracker's outsider optimiser uses the same post-transcendence shape: `log10(HS) = log10(1+TP)·(HZE − 100)/5 + log10(1 + ponyBonus) + log10(20 · series · pbcm)` (i.e. HS ≈ 20·(1+TP)^((HZE−100)/5)·(1+Pony)·[primal-boss series]), and `hsMultiplier = (1 + TP)^0.2` per zone. — [outsiderSuggestions.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/components/outsiderSuggestions/outsiderSuggestions.ts)

**What souls buy: ancients and their cost curves** – [source code] Ancient cost functions: "one" = `n` per level (Morgulis: +11% DPS per level), "linear" = `n(n+1)/2` (triangular; Siyalatas, Argaiv, Libertas, Mammon, Mimzee, Bhaal, Fragsworth…), "polynomial1_5" = `Σ i^1.5` (Solomon), "exponential" = `2^(n+1) − 1` (Kumawakamaru, Chronos, Atman, Bubos, Dora, Dogcog, Fortuna, Revolc, Vaagur — these have hard caps, e.g. Atman 2880, Chronos 1101, Kuma 1498). Rules-of-thumb ratios: Argaiv = Siyalatas; Morgulis = Siyalatas²; Mammon = Mimzee = 0.926·Siyalatas; Libertas = Mammon; Bhaal = Fragsworth (active); Juggernaut = primary^0.8. — [ancientSuggestions.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/components/ancientSuggestions/ancientSuggestions.ts)

**Transcendence (second prestige layer)** – [search summary, official blog] `Ancient Souls = floor(5 · log10(total Hero Souls ever sacrificed))` — every ×10 in lifetime souls gives +5 AS (10,000 HS → 20 AS; 100,000 HS → 25 AS). Transcending sacrifices hero souls, gilds, ancients, relics and zone progress; it is "normally available after zone 300". Transcendent Power formula quoted by the blog: `TP% = 50 − 49·e^(−AS/10000)`, minimum 1% after the first transcension. — [Clicker Heroes blog: Ancient Souls](https://blog.clickerheroes.com/clicker-heroes-ancient-souls-master-the-ultimate-currency/), [blog: Transcendence guide](https://blog.clickerheroes.com/clicker-heroes-transcendence-guide-outsiders-souls-explained/)
- [source code] The Tracker (older game version) uses `transcendentPower = (25 − 23·e^(−0.0003·AS))/100` and `newAncientSouls = max(AS, floor(5·log10(HS)))`; outsider level cost `= (level+1)·level/2`. The two TP formulas differ (blog vs tracker) — they belong to different game versions; both give TP ≈ 1–2% at 0 AS and saturate (25% resp. 50%). — [outsiderSuggestions.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/components/outsiderSuggestions/outsiderSuggestions.ts)
- [source code] Ancient optimiser relates the HP growth base to transcendent power via `hpScale = floor(HZE/500)·0.005 + 1.145` and `alpha = ln(1+TP)·1.4067 / ln(hpScale)` — i.e. the game's own balance tooling explicitly compares the soul-growth exponent to the monster-HP exponent. — [ancientSuggestions.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/components/ancientSuggestions/ancientSuggestions.ts)

**Design intent (Pecorella on Clicker Heroes)** – [article mirror] "Clicker Heroes … has a very steep difficulty curve that makes progression through the main series of 'hero zones' fairly slow. As a result, the ability to keep earning prestige currency at about the same point gives players the ability to progress despite a pretty substantial progress wall. The currency itself is based on the number of upgrades purchased for the generators (heroes). Because the cost of upgrades grows at an exponential rate, this does effectively 'take a log' of the overall growth curve." — [Pecorella, Math of Idle Games (Kongregate/GameAnalytics reprint, GitHub mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md); original: [Game Developer, Part III](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-iii)

### Inferences
- Worked HP table (computed by me from the cited `monsterLifeFormula1`; values are the *base* HP before the boss multiplier — every zone listed except z1 is a multiple of 5 and therefore a boss zone whose actual HP is ×10, or ×10.08 at z1000; ≈ values): z1 = 10; z10 ≈ 6.1e2 (boss ≈ 6.1e3); z25 ≈ 3.7e5 (boss ≈ 3.7e6); z50 ≈ 2.1e10; z100 ≈ 7.0e19; z140 ≈ 2.9e27; z200 ≈ 9.7e30; z300 ≈ 7.3e36; z500 ≈ 4.2e48; z1000 ≈ 1.7e78 (boss ≈ 1.7e79). Per 10 zones the early regime is ×79.6, the mid regime ×3.87 — Fantasy Idle's ×1.15/stage (×4.05 per 10 stages) sits right on CH's mid-game slope.
- Gold-to-HP ratio: 1/15 up to zone 75, rising to 3/15 = 1/5 by zone ≈120 (1.025^44.5 = 3) and flat after. Gold therefore grows at exactly the enemy-HP rate, which is what lets hero purchases (cost ×1.07/level) keep pace: one zone's HP growth (×1.145) buys ≈ ln(1.145)/ln(1.07) ≈ 2.0 hero levels of cost growth.
- Why 1.07 vs 1.145/1.15 is "balanced": hero DPS per level is linear but every 25 levels gets a ×4 multiplier (×4^(1/25) ≈ ×1.057/level averaged) while cost is ×1.07/level, so DPS-per-gold decays ~1.2%/level (the wiki's 0.988 figure). Within a run, DPS therefore grows a little slower than HP and the run stalls gradually rather than abruptly; the +10%/soul multiplier and the ancients shift the whole curve so the next run stalls later. This is my reading of the cited numbers, not a developer statement.
- Post-transcendence, souls per primal boss are exponential in zone with base (1+TP)^(1/5) per zone; because DPS is linear in souls, DPS from prestige is itself exponential in HZE — the same *shape* as monster HP, so the prestige layer can never be permanently outrun. TP (the exponent base) itself grows with log of lifetime souls, which is the "second derivative" that keeps the loop alive over months.

## KQ2. Melvor Idle combat mechanics (hit chance, max hit, XP, food/auto-eat, potions, drops, dungeons, death, offline)

### Takeaway
Melvor's combat is a small deterministic stat system: accuracy/evasion = (effectiveLevel+9)·(bonus+64); hit chance = 1 − 0.5·evasion/accuracy when accuracy ≥ evasion (else 0.5·accuracy/evasion); melee/ranged max hit = floor(10·(1.3 + L/10 + B/80 + L·B/640)) with L = level+9; damage reduction is capped at 95% and multiplied by a combat-triangle factor; XP is 0.133 Hitpoints XP per point of damage (plus the attack style's 0.4/damage); auto-eat triggers at a threshold % of max HP and refills to a higher % with a per-tier efficiency; death sets HP to 20%, deletes one random equipped item (unless Protect Item) and stops combat; offline play is a literal tick-by-tick simulation (50 ms ticks) capped at 24 h, so running out of food offline simply results in the same death and combat stopping.

### Cited Findings

**Stat formulas (all [source code], de-obfuscated Melvor source dump, `src/character.js`)** — [character.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/character.js)
- `static calculateStandardStat(values) { return (values.effectiveLevel + 9) * (values.bonus + 64); }` — used for accuracy rating and each evasion rating (melee/ranged/magic) with the matching level and equipment bonus.
- `static calculateStandardMaxHit(baseLevel, strengthBonus) { const effectiveLevel = baseLevel + 9; return Math.floor(numberMultiplier * (1.3 + effectiveLevel/10 + strengthBonus/80 + (effectiveLevel*strengthBonus)/640)); }`
- Magic max hit: `Math.floor(numberMultiplier * spell.maxHit * (1 + magicDamageBonus/100) * (1 + (levels.Magic + 1)/200))`.
- Min hit: `minHit += Math.floor(maxHit * minHitFromMaxHitPercent / 100)` (plus flat modifiers ×numberMultiplier).
- Hit chance: `if (accuracy < targetEvasion) hitchance = (0.5*accuracy/targetEvasion)*100; else hitchance = (1 − 0.5*targetEvasion/accuracy)*100;`
- Damage reduction: `damageReduction = clampValue(reduction, 0, 95)` after the triangle modifier.
- Max HP: `maxHP = numberMultiplier * levels.Hitpoints` (+ flat modifiers, minimum `numberMultiplier`).
- Attack interval floor: `attackInterval = Math.max(attackInterval, 250)` ms.
- `numberMultiplier` is `10` by default and is set to the gamemode's `hitpointMultiplier` on load (`let numberMultiplier = 10;` … `numberMultiplier = game.currentGamemode.hitpointMultiplier;`). — [main.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/main.js)
- Enemies use the *same* Character formulas with levels and "equipment stats" taken straight from monster data: `computeLevels() { Object.assign(this.levels, this.monster.levels) }`, `computeEquipmentStats() { this.equipmentStats.addStats(this.monster.equipmentStats) }`. — [enemy.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/enemy.js)
- The same two hit-chance branches were reported (and the roll `playerAccuracy > Math.floor(Math.random()*100)`) in a 2021 bug report on the official issue tracker. — [MelvorIdle/melvoridle.github.io issue #609](https://github.com/MelvorIdle/melvoridle.github.io/issues/609)
- [search summary] Wiki prose version: "Accuracy Rating = floor((Effective Skill Level + 9) × (Base Accuracy Bonus + 64) × (1 + Accuracy Modifier/100))"; equal ratings → 50% hit; double → 75%; triple → 83.3%. — [Melvor wiki: Attack/Boosts](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Attack/Boosts), [Combat](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Combat)

**Combat triangle** – [source code] `combatTriangle.js` (index 0 is the `"Standard"` triangle referenced by gamemode data; index 1 is, by elimination, Hardcore; index 2 an inverted variant used by a special mode):
```
[0] damageModifier:   melee{melee 1, ranged 1.1, magic 0.85}  ranged{melee 0.85, ranged 1, magic 1.1}  magic{melee 1.1, ranged 0.85, magic 1}
    reductionModifier: melee{melee 1, ranged 1.25, magic 0.75} ranged{melee 0.95, ranged 1, magic 1.25} magic{melee 1.25, ranged 0.85, magic 1}
[1] damageModifier:   melee{1, 1.1, 0.75}  ranged{0.75, 1, 1.1}  magic{1.1, 0.75, 1}
    reductionModifier: melee{1, 1.25, 0.5}  ranged{0.75, 1, 1.25}  magic{1.25, 0.75, 1}
[2] (inverted) damage melee{1, 0.75, 1.1} ranged{1.1, 1, 0.75} magic{0.75, 1.1, 1}; reduction melee{1, 0.75, 1.25} ranged{1.25, 1, 0.75} magic{0.5, 1.25, 1}
```
Applied as `damage *= activeTriangle.damageModifier[attackType][target.attackType]` and `reduction *= activeTriangle.reductionModifier[attackType][target.attackType]` only while a fight is in progress. — [combatTriangle.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/combatTriangle.js), [player.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/player.js)
- A community DR compendium lists slightly different DR modifiers (Melee vs Magic 0.5×, Hardcore 0.25×; Ranged vs Melee 0.95× / HC 0.75×; Magic vs Ranged 0.85× / HC 0.75×) — probably an older game version; it also states the idle-survival rule `PlayerHP × AutoEatThreshold > MaxHit × (1 − DR)` (with DR × triangle modifier in dungeons). — [gridster2/Melvor Compendium of Damage Reduction](https://github.com/gridster2/Melvor/blob/main/Documents/Compendium%20of%20Damage%20Reduction.md)

**Combat XP** – [source code] `rewardXPAndPetsForDamage(damage)`: `damage = damage / numberMultiplier;` then each entry of the attack style's `experienceGain` gets `gain.ratio * damage`, Hitpoints gets `damage * 1.33`, Prayer gets `(Σ pointsPerPlayer / 3) * damage`. With numberMultiplier = 10 that is 0.133 HP XP per displayed damage point. — [player.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/player.js)
- [search summary] Wiki: "0.133 XP is awarded per point of damage" to Hitpoints for every style; "0.4 XP is awarded per point of damage done to enemies for Strength" (attack-style skill); prayer 1/30 per damage per prayer point. — [Melvor wiki: Hitpoints](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Hitpoints), [Strength](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/index.php/Strength)

**Food and Auto Eat** – [source code] (`player.js`):
- `autoEatThreshold = maxHitpoints * min(100, increasedAutoEatThreshold − decreasedAutoEatThreshold)/100`; `autoEatHPLimit = maxHitpoints * min(100, …HPLimit…)/100`; `autoEatEfficiency = max(increasedAutoEatEfficiency − decreasedAutoEatEfficiency, 1)`.
- `autoEat()`: when `hitpoints <= autoEatThreshold` (or food was just swapped) and a food slot is non-empty: `autoEatHealing = floor(getFoodHealing(item) * autoEatEfficiency / 100)`; `foodQty = ceil((autoEatHPLimit − hitpoints) / autoEatHealing)`, capped at slot quantity; eats that many; if the slot is now empty and `autoSwapFoodUnlocked` (+ setting), switches to the next non-empty food slot and eats again.
- `getFoodHealing(item) = applyModifier(item.healsFor * numberMultiplier, foodHealingBonus)`; `eatFood(qty, interrupt, efficiency)` heals `qty * max(floor(healing*efficiency/100), 1)`.
- Regen: `regen = maxHitpoints/100` (+flat) every `10000 − decreasedRegenerationInterval` ms.
- The Golbin-Raid player is hard-coded with `increasedAutoEatThreshold: 30, increasedAutoEatEfficiency: 80, increasedAutoEatHPLimit: 60` — an example of a "tier" triple (threshold %, efficiency %, refill-to %). — [raidPlayer.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/raidPlayer.js)

You lost nothing."; lost ammo/tablets lose the whole stack; Protect Item prayer prevents loss; Hardcore characters are deleted. — [Melvor wiki: FAQ](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/FAQ), [Combat](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Combat)

**Offline combat** – [source code] `getOfflineTimeDiff()`: `timeDiff = min(now − tickTimestamp, 86400000)` (24 h cap). `processOffline()`: if the active action is combat and `!settings.enableOfflineCombat`, or the area is a Slayer area while Slayer is locked, combat is stopped first; otherwise the game runs `runTicks(floor(timeDiff / TICK_INTERVAL))` with `TICK_INTERVAL = 50` ms — a genuine replay of every tick (kills, drops, food, potion charges, death) — then shows a summary modal. Because death during the replay goes through the same `checkDeath()` → `stop(false)`, an offline session in which food runs out ends at the moment of death (item lost, HP 20%) and no further progress accrues. — [game.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/game.js), [combatManager.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/combatManager.js)
- [search summary] Wiki: the offline cap "started at 12 hours and was raised over time to 24"; "Combat and Thieving cannot be done offline until the 'Toggle Offline Combat' setting has been enabled"; players are advised to ensure enough food because "you'll die"; the offline summary reports food eaten per hour. — [Melvor wiki: Offline Progression](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Offline_Progression), [Steam thread on offline auto-eat](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/discussions/0/707749927473812598/)

### Inferences
- Idle-safety design rule (derived from the cited auto-eat code): a player can idle a monster indefinitely iff `maxHP · threshold% ≥ monsterMaxHit · (1 − DR·triangleMod)` (the compendium states the same). Efficiency only changes the food burn rate: food per hour ≈ (damage taken per hour) / (healsFor·10·efficiency). This is a clean, testable pair of formulas for Fantasy Idle's auto-heal.
- Because Melvor enemies use the identical stat pipeline as the player and their "levels" are hand-authored per monster (no per-stage exponent), difficulty is curated content: areas → slayer areas → dungeons → god dungeons. Endless scaling is absent by design; replay value comes from drop tables (rare uniques) and mastery/skill levels, not from an infinite stage counter.
- Melvor's max-hit formula is quadratic in (level × strength bonus)/640, so gear and levels multiply rather than add — a cheap way to get super-linear player growth from two linear inputs.

### Gaps
- The per-monster table (HP, levels, max hit, attack speed, GP min/max, drop chance and table) and the dungeon table (monster counts, rewards) live in the game's data JSON, which is not in the source dump and the wiki was blocked; no rows could be transcribed.
- Auto Eat shop tiers: only the refill-to values (40/60/80%) and Tier I efficiency (60%) surfaced; the trigger thresholds and GP costs per tier were not captured.
- Slayer-area requirements and the typical attack-interval range (e.g. 2.4–4.0 s) were not captured.

## KQ3. Enemy scaling and prestige formulas in Tap Titans 2, Idle Slayer, NGU Idle, Realm Grinder, Antimatter Dimensions (plus AdCap, Cookie Clicker, Egg Inc.)

### Takeaway
Every long-lived idle game makes the prestige currency a fractional power or exponential of *progress* (stage, lifetime earnings, antimatter), then spends it on multiplicative, mostly linear-cost upgrades, so total power grows in step with an exponential enemy curve; the specific exponents (√, ∛, ^0.14, ^1.75, 10^(x/308)) set how much farther each run must go to double the prestige gain (4×, 8×, 128× …).

### Cited Findings

**Tap Titans 2 (2017 server variables, TT2-Sim)** — [source code] [ServerVarsModel.py](https://github.com/metxchris/TT2-Sim/blob/ef0e375a8a876b336ac893a1240f5d9baf776533/ServerVarsModel.py), [DataClasses.py](https://github.com/metxchris/TT2-Sim/blob/ef0e375a8a876b336ac893a1240f5d9baf776533/DataClasses.py)
- Titan HP: `titan_hp = monsterHPMultiplier · monsterHPBase1^min(stage, monsterHPLevelOff) · monsterHPBase2^max(stage − monsterHPLevelOff, 0) · hp_multiplier` with `monsterHPMultiplier = 17.5`, `monsterHPBase1 = 1.39`, `monsterHPBase2 = 1.13`, `monsterHPLevelOff = 115`.
- Boss HP: `boss_hp = titan_hp · min(bossHPModBase^(stage/200), 2.5)` with `bossHPModBase = 1.1`, then multiplied by `themeMultiplierSequence = [2, 3, 4, 5, 8]` cycling by stage mod 5 (stages x1,x6 → ×2 … x5,x0 → ×8).
- Titans per stage: `max(1, 10 + min(floor(stage/1000)·4, 20) − LessMonsters)` (`monsterCountBase = 10`, `monsterCountInc = 4`, `maxMonsterCount = 30`); boss timer `30 + skill bonus` s.
- Gold: `base_titan_gold = titan_hp · monsterGoldMultiplier + monsterGoldSlope · min(stage, noMoreMonsterGoldSlope)` with `monsterGoldMultiplier = 0.008`, `monsterGoldSlope = 0.0002`, `noMoreMonsterGoldSlope = 150`; stage-0 gold hard-set to 30 "to purchase first hero"; boss gold uses boss HP and `maxBossGoldMultiplier = 3`; chest ("Chesterson") chance 1% with `treasureGold = 10`; ×10-gold proc 0.5%.
- Relics on prestige: `relics = BookOfShadows_mult · ((max(0, stage − 75)/14)^1.75)` (`relicFromStageBaseline = 75`, `relicFromStageDivider = 14`, `relicFromStagePower = 1.75`), plus `relicFromHelperLevel = 1000` (1 relic per 1000 hero levels); `prestigeLevelReq = 600` (prestige unlocks at stage 600); `relicBossDropChance = 0.3`.
- Costs: hero level cost grows `helperUpgradeBase = 1.082` per level (bulk cost `purchase_cost · 1.082^level · (1.082^n − 1)/(1.082 − 1)`); Sword Master `playerUpgradeCostBase = 5`, growth `1.062`; artifacts' relic value `Σ cost_coef · i^cost_expo`.
- Active play constants: `tapsPerSecond = 20`, `playerCritChance = 0.002` (max 0.5), crit multiplier 3–20×, `tap_damage = sword_master_damage + tap_from_hero · total_hero_dps`.
- [search summary] A later-version FAQ gives a re-tuned curve: `MaxHP = 18.5 · 1.57^min(stage,150) · 1.17^max(stage−150,0)`, `BossMaxHP = MaxHP · [2,4,6,7,10]` chosen by `(Stage − 1) mod 5`, "(1 − bonuses)" from HP-reducing artifacts. — [Tap Titans 2 wiki: FAQ](https://tap-titans-2.fandom.com/wiki/FAQ)
- [search summary] Current relic rule: relics from prestige = hero-level bonus (1 per 1000 hired hero levels) + stage-completion bonus that steps up every 15 stages (90, 105, 120, …, larger per step at higher stages; reaching the stage suffices) + "Full Team Bonus" (duplicate if all heroes alive); a prestige potion duplicates again. — [Tap Titans wiki: Prestige](https://tap-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Prestige), [Relics](https://tap-titans.fandom.com/wiki/Relics)

**Idle Slayer** — [search summary]
- Souls needed for one Slayer Point: `12 + 4 · LifetimeSlayerPoints` (each SP costs 4 more souls than the previous; the first costs 12). — [Idle Slayer wiki: Ascension Strategy](https://idleslayer.fandom.com/wiki/Ascension_Strategy)

Souls per kill scale with earned SP via the Soul Reaper ascension node. Ultra Ascension grants Ultra Slayer Points based on lifetime SP (total + unclaimed). — [Ascension Strategy](https://idleslayer.fandom.com/wiki/Ascension_Strategy), [Ultra Slayer Points](https://idleslayer.fandom.com/wiki/Ultra_Slayer_Points)

### Inferences
- Classification of the prestige formulas by "what must grow for the reward to double" (my computation from the cited exponents): Egg Inc. (^0.14) 128× run earnings; Cookie Clicker (∛) 8× lifetime; AdCap/Realm Grinder (√) ~4×; TT2 2017 relics (stage^1.75) only 2^(1/1.75) = 1.49× the stage-distance-above-75 (i.e. going from stage 375 to 522 doubles relics); Clicker Heroes post-transcendence (exponential in HZE) a *constant* +Δzone = 5·ln2/ln(1+TP) (≈175 zones at TP 2%, ≈36 zones at TP 10%); AD (10^(log AM/308)) needs antimatter^(1/308)… i.e. 2^308 ≈ 10^93 more antimatter to double IP, which is why AD adds multiplier upgrades and a second layer (EP) instead of relying on the base exponent.
- TT2's gold rule (`0.008·HP + small slope`) and Clicker Heroes' (`HP/15`) are the same design: gold per kill ∝ enemy HP, so income and difficulty share one exponent and hero costs (×1.082 or ×1.07 per level) are always affordable in log-time.

### Gaps
- Idle Slayer "ascension shards" / "power": no such mechanic was found under those names; Idle Slayer's currencies are Souls → Slayer Points (Ascension Tree), Ultra Slayer Points, and Divinities. The caller may be thinking of another game.
- NGU: the explicit per-factor NUMBER formula (boss-count exponent etc.) and the game's number-format options were not captured.
- Realm Grinder: the per-gem production bonus percentage was not captured.
- TT2 current-version relic-per-stage numbers and artifact cost curves were not captured (only the 2017 constants and the qualitative "every 15 stages" rule).

## KQ4. Design rules: why an exponential enemy vs polynomial player produces a permanent wall, and the standard fixes

### Takeaway
Pecorella's core statement is that exponential growth "will eventually catch and far exceed any polynomial growth no matter how big k is or how small n is"; idle games deliberately put costs/enemies on the exponential and production on the polynomial so every run stalls, then use a prestige multiplier to "slide the curve up" — so the *prestige* term must itself grow exponentially in progress (or the enemy curve must bend) or the game walls permanently.

### Cited Findings
- [article mirror] "At the most basic level, idle games are a seesaw between production rates and costs. Early on in a run, your production will exceed costs while eventually costs will become prohibitive. This is typically accomplished by having costs grow exponentially while production grows at a linear or polynomial rate": `cost_next = cost_base × rate_growth^owned`, `production_total = (production_base × owned) × multipliers`; AdCap Lemonade Stand `rate_growth = 1.07`, `cost_base = 4`, `production_base = 1.67/sec`. — [Pecorella Part I (mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md); original [Game Developer Part I](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i)
- [article mirror] "Mathematically, exponential growth (anything of the form n^x, for n > 1) will eventually catch and far exceed any polynomial growth (x^k) no matter how big k is or how small n is. … once things really start to slow down, you would be looking to do a prestige to multiply your production rate. … each prestige allows the player to get farther through the cost curve before falling behind." (Pecorella links Eclipse1agg's r/incremental_games post "Linear, Polynomial, Exponential (and more) growth explained" for growth tiers.) — [Part I (mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md); [r/incremental_games growth post](https://www.reddit.com/r/incremental_games/comments/2ztcfk/linear_polynomial_exponential_and_more_growth/) (not fetched)
- [article mirror] Multiplier milestones "(stacking) x2 multiplier" at 25 and 50 owned create purchase spikes and are "temporarily overvalued"; varying milestone multipliers per generator makes "different generators take priority" over time. — [Part I (mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md)
- [article mirror] Bulk-buy closed forms for exponential costs: `cost = b · r^k (r^n − 1)/(r − 1)`, `max = floor(log_r(c(r−1)/(b·r^k) + 1))`. — [Part I (mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md)
- [article mirror] Derivative growth (generators producing generators) approaches exponential: `1, x, x²/2, x³/6, …, x^n/n!` → e^x; Derivative Clicker costs `5 × 1.1^n` while growth is sub-exponential, "which is perfect"; purchased-count tier boosts (+0.05% per purchased tier-1 building) keep cheap tiers relevant. — [Part II (mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md); original [Game Developer Part II](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/the-math-of-idle-games-part-ii)
- [article mirror] Prestige serves two purposes: "the 'ladder climbing' effect" and "They rein growth back into a more manageable number … since growth here will be slower." Correction: "Most prestiges are a fractional exponent (e.g. a square or cube root) rather than a mathematical log function … Clicker Heroes is an exception that really does have a log-type effect … some of these games have since added systems that do substantially rein in numbers, such as Realm Grinder's Reincarnations and AdVenture Capitalist's Mega Bucks." — [Part III (mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md)
- [article mirror] Lifetime-based vs since-reset prestige: since-reset systems "must be balanced quite differently. They have very flat curves … getting farther in a run has rapidly diminished returns" and "can create ideal strategies that don't involve much progress in the game beyond a certain point"; lifetime systems let you "keep resetting at the same point and gain currency … the amount of currency diminishes each time, which means that players do need to be able to advance to make appreciable gains." — [Part III (mirror)](https://github.com/manhonggang/Notes/blob/05a4d8834cf3b04a781c9a480dc58f0898c63ba3/%E8%A7%81%E9%97%BB%E8%B5%84%E6%96%99/The%20Math%20Behind%20Idle%20Games%20(A%20Study%20by%20Kongregate)%20-%20GameAnalytics.md)

- [source code] Zone-gated finite content with authored numbers (no endless exponent) is Melvor's approach: monsters carry fixed `levels`/`equipmentStats`, dungeons are ordered lists with completion rewards. — [enemy.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/enemy.js), [combatManager.js](https://github.com/ChanceToZoinks/melvor-source/blob/695f1b1b9f886b6f492958e8182a699563afd9e1/src/combatManager.js)

### Inferences (applying the cited rules to Fantasy Idle — my derivation, not from a source)
- Stall-point model: with enemy growth g_e per stage and in-run player growth g_p per stage (g_p < g_e), a permanent multiplier M moves the stall forward by `Δstage = ln(M) / ln(g_e / g_p)`. For a loop that keeps giving the same +Δ per run, `ln(M_next / M_prev)` must be constant → total prestige multiplier must be exponential in best stage, `M(S) ∝ b^S`. Any polynomial token formula spent on doubling-cost upgrades violates this.
- Fantasy Idle today: tokens `T = floor(((S−1)/10)^1.5)` → S=101: 31 tokens; S=201: 89; S=501: 353; S=1001: 1000. With +10%/level at doubling costs (total cost of L levels = 2^L − 1), `L ≈ log2(T)`, so `M = 1.1^L ≈ T^0.1375 ∝ S^0.206`: ×1.61 at S=101, ×2.36 at S=1001. Enemy HP over the same span grows ×1.15^900 ≈ 10^54. Gear is ~linear (≤1200 ATK). This is the textbook permanent wall.
- Sizing the fix with the stall model and Fantasy Idle's numbers (g_e = 1.15 HP, g_p ≈ 1 for linear gear): Δstage = ln(M)/0.1398 → ×2 buys +5 stages, ×4.05 buys +10, ×10 buys +16.5, ×100 buys +33. To extend each run by ≈10 stages the *total* multiplier must ×4.05 per run; with linear-cost +10% levels that is +14.7 levels per run, so tokens should be roughly exponential in stage (e.g. `T(S) = k · 4^(S/10) − spent` so that a run ending 10 stages further always affords ~15 more levels), or the bonus per token should be exponential/multiplicative-stacking (CH's +10%/soul with souls exponential in zone). Alternatively bend the enemy curve (CH/TT2-style regime change to ~1.13–1.145 or a log soft-cap) and/or give gear an exponential tier ladder (Melvor-style level×bonus/640 quadratic term plus prestige multipliers).
- Multiplicative stacking of independent bonus sources (CH: achievements × Dark Ritual × all-hero × hero-souls × level milestones × gilds; Melvor: level × strength bonus cross term; TT2: hero_multiplier × all_damage × all_hero_damage × weapon_bonus) is how these games turn several linear systems into super-exponential player growth; additive stacking would leave growth polynomial.

## KQ5. Prestige timing: first-prestige targets, run-length evolution, what is kept and why, common mistakes

### Takeaway
Documented targets cluster around "first reset within the first hour or two of play, then runs lengthening as the prestige gain from repeating the same point flattens": Clicker Heroes players are steered to ascend once they reach zone ~130 (guaranteed primal bosses at 110/120/130), Idle Slayer's wiki says ascend when the new points would add 10–20% to your lifetime total, NGU Idle penalises rebirths shorter than 60 minutes down to 0.00031% at 3 minutes, and Pecorella's model shows why: with √-type formulas you must earn ~4× more to double the reward, so the gain from resetting at the same point decays toward zero and the player is forced to push farther each run.

### Cited Findings
- [article mirror] "Since this formula is based on the square root of the max currency earned, it means that if you reset at the same point you will earn literally no more prestige currency. - [article mirror] On run length and variation: "in the same way that you want to make progress through a run somewhat 'bumpy' with slow parts and fast parts, you likely want to have similar variation in your prestige resets too"; his spreadsheet example shows resets every few tens of minutes with "a fairly long prestige starting at 120 minutes. ### Inferences
- "Too weak" prestige (nothing changes after a reset) is the failure Pecorella's flat-curve warning describes; "too strong" (resetting at the same point keeps paying) is the CH/Egg-Inc since-reset failure mode where the optimal strategy is to farm one point forever — CH mitigates it by making souls per primal boss exponential in zone (so pushing one boss further is always worth more) while Egg Inc. accepts it because progress comes from a separate 5× sub-prestige ladder.
- A useful control knob visible in the sources is a *floor/threshold* on progress before prestige pays (CH's zone 100 for primal bosses and the (zone−80) offset; TT2's stage 75 baseline and stage 600 gate; Realm Grinder's first gem at 10^12). Keeping a start-stage bonus is fine, but it does not substitute for an exponential multiplier — it only removes waiting time, and if gold/loot are stage-linked it also removes early income.

## KQ6. Combat rewards: gold-per-kill relative to HP, loot table design, boss rewards, farming lower stages

### Takeaway
Both endless-stage games read here derive gold directly from enemy HP (Clicker Heroes `ceil(HP/15 · zoneBonus ≤ 3)`, Tap Titans 2 `0.008·HP + 0.0002·min(stage,150)`) with bosses and rare chests as multiplied variants (TT2 boss gold ≤ ×3, chests ×10 at 1%; CH chests ×10 at 1%), whereas Melvor uses hand-authored per-monster GP ranges plus a per-monster loot chance and weighted drop table; because gold ∝ HP, farming an earlier stage in CH/TT2 is exponentially worse per kill, so the only reason to farm is prestige-currency-from-purchases (CH's 1 soul per 2,000 hero levels), which Pecorella flags as the "since-reset" strategy problem.

### Cited Findings
- [source code] CH gold: `ceil(oldHP/15 · goldMultiplier · chestMult · (1 + ancientGold%) · min(3, 1.025^(zone−75)))`; chest ×10 at 1% chance; boss gold has no separate multiplier in the model (bosses are just ×10-HP monsters, so they drop ~10× gold). — [monster.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/models/monster.ts), [userData.ts](https://github.com/dfederm/ClickerHeroesTracker/blob/fe535216fa41111c9b531c189e3d9afae324229b/WebClient/src/models/userData.ts)

- [article mirror] "This means that if you reset at the same point a few times in a row you will get the same amount of currency each time. - [article mirror] Pecorella's "Primary Exchange Currency" concept: "in Clicker Heroes the generators produce DPS, which is then converted into gold by killing monsters. ### Inferences
- For Fantasy Idle (currently no gold or loot from combat) the minimal CH/TT2-style rule is `gold_per_kill = ceil(enemyHP / k · stageBonus)` with k ≈ 15 (CH) or 125 (TT2) and a capped early-stage ramp (CH: ×1.025 per stage after 75, cap ×3); this makes crafting costs and enemy HP share one exponent, so gear costs can also be exponential per tier without ever becoming unaffordable in log-time.
- If loot should feel Melvor-like, keep a per-enemy `lootChance` + weighted table with a rarity tier whose weights are stage-independent, and put stage scaling into gold/material *quantity* rather than drop chance — this keeps farming a lower stage viable for specific drops (Melvor's model) while gold still pulls the player forward (CH/TT2's model).

## KQ7. Auto-progress, boss cadence, zone structure, and death/retreat rules

### Takeaway
Endless-stage games use a short boss cadence (every 5 zones in Clicker Heroes and Tap Titans 2, each boss ~10×/2–8× a normal monster with a 30 s timer) and treat failure as costless (fall back and farm); Melvor instead has no stages at all — curated areas plus dungeons that are ordered monster lists whose progress resets if you leave or die, with death costing one random equipped item (or the whole save in Hardcore).

### Inferences
- Fantasy Idle's "boss every 10 stages, death drops one stage" is closest to CH's "fail the 30-s boss → keep farming below it" model; the sources suggest the death penalty in an endless-stage game should be time, not stage/loot (CH/TT2 lose nothing), and that a *timer* rather than HP-attrition is what makes a boss a check on DPS.
