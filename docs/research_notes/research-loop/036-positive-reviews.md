# 036: What idle RPG players praise (read 11 November 2026)

**Sample.** 035's ten games, on each game's Steam community pages: the 50 most helpful positive English reviews of all time
([Melvor's](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/positivereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english)), then the 50 most recent not already taken
([Melvor's](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/positivereviews/?browsefilter=mostrecent&filterLanguage=english)), loaded through the pages' own load-more
requests; the same two pages for each game below (in four games, 10 of the 60 most recent were already taken). 1,000 reviews, 20,077 helpful votes. Hours at
review come from each review's own page; 142 show only the total, as Steam does when no play followed, so the total was used. Steam counts only its own client,
so the 29 reviews under half an hour include players of other versions ([one](https://steamcommunity.com/id/vampire337/recommended/1013320/) had 3,337 hours on
Epic). Totals are Steam's histograms (all languages), within 2 of 035's.

**Coding, and by whom.** The codebook below was written before reading: several praise themes a review and one main praise; then the faults the same writer
names, in 035's codebook; a stance (plain, mixed, reluctant); flags for a joke, quitting and coming back. Ten Claude subagents, one per game, read every review
in full and coded it by judgment. I coded 50 random reviews (five a game) blind before opening their codes: the main praise matched in 45, theme sets overlapped
0.85 on average (Jaccard), faults 0.95, stance in 46; the coders' reports had named two of the 50 first. Word counts run only inside a theme coded by judgment,
and are floors. Nine checklist templates (copied grading boxes) and ten copies of other reviews are kept; the templates give 7 of the 20 sound praises.

**The codebook.** Treatment: idle (plays itself, offline, background, a busy life), money (free, fair, no ads, not pay-to-win), progress, content, relax,
hooked (addictive, as praise). Systems: depth (interlocking systems, strategy, builds), active, automation, loot, collect, prestige, combat, skills. Look: art,
humour (with writing and lore), sound, ui (interface, readouts, accessibility). People and platform: dev (the developer's conduct), updates, social (community,
clans, co-op, market), like (a loved game as a positive comparison), tech (polish, performance), platform (cross-play, cloud saves). Plus other and none.

**Limits.** A share is what pleased writers name. 300 of 1,000 give no reason (19% of the helpful half, 41% of the recent) against 79 of 035's negatives, so
praise is undercounted. The helpful half is older and heavier (median 183 hours at review, the recent 99); bands: 199 under 10 hours, 269 at 10–100, 532 over.
Four of NGU's 50 recent positives mention a cat in the game that asks for a review ([one](https://steamcommunity.com/id/Manofmanybuds/recommended/1147690/)). No review held
instructions aimed at an AI; a few ask readers for likes or awards, one with 293 votes ([review](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198984996559/recommended/1353300/)). Fantasy Idle: this round's worktree.

## In short

1. **Players praise how a game treats them more than what is in it.** Of 1,000: it respects their time (179), it is free or fair (177), progress is steady
   (168), it hooks them (158), it plays like a game they loved (139). Combat (29), loot (26), skills (17) and prestige (11) are each under 3%.
2. **Praise and fault fall on the same things, with different weight.** At the ten games' 4.85 positives per negative (95,004 to 19,590), idle play is praised
   7 times per fault, the likeness to another game 10, art, writing and sound 10; money 2.5, the developer 2.1, bugs 1.2. Inside a game money tips over: 0.5
   praise per fault in Milky Way Idle, 0.8 in IdleOn, 1.0 in Firestone, against 6.8 in Melvor and 24 in USI. Stability, the developer and updates work as
   hygiene: praised by 3%, 6% and 6%, faulted by 12%, 15% and 9% (035).
3. **By hours:** under 10, the likeness and idle play lead (16% each) and 41% give no reason. Over 100: progress 21%, money 20%, hooked 19%, depth 17%;
   the developer and updates together rise from 6% to 14%.
4. **A quarter of positives also fault something** (253: grind 73, money 68, boring 49). IdleOn's two most-voted positives (635 and 328 votes) praise nothing
   and fault its developer and shop.
5. **RuneScape without the chores, Melvor with friends:** RuneScape is named in 52 likenesses (28 also praise idle play), Melvor in 32, all Idle Clans' or Milky
   Way Idle's, 20 of them praising the multiplayer it adds.
6. **For Fantasy Idle:** the top praises are in place, and its listing says the first two (`index.html:13`). It lacks music, chat and a market (the last two
   by choice), a word on what changed, readouts that show what isn't working, and full play without hands.

## What the praise holds

- **Idle play is about lives, not mechanics:** of 179, at least 47 name a background tab or second screen, 36 work, school or family, 34 time away. Two of
  the ten most-voted positives (602 votes) are about bodies: no use of the hands ([375 votes](https://steamcommunity.com/id/JoyeauxNoel/recommended/627690/)),
  or one hand, a baby in the other arm, and no call to log in every 15 minutes ([227 votes](https://steamcommunity.com/id/codeGlaze/recommended/627690/)).
- **Money praise is mostly about being free:** of 177, 104 say free or free-to-play, 48 a price, 37 not pay-to-win, 6 ads. In cash-shop games it defends the
  shop: Idle Clans' premium can be bought with gold earned in play ([82 votes](https://steamcommunity.com/id/Mtkx11/recommended/2103530/)).
- **The likeness:** "It's like RuneScape except you don't have to play it" ([Melvor, 207 votes](https://steamcommunity.com/id/ancientpower/recommended/1267910/));
  Idle Clans as Melvor with less but with multiplayer, and a plea for sound ([5.8 h](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198025262330/recommended/2103530/)).
- **Readouts:** USI's players name a stats screen that shows the speed-ups not in use, so no Discord is needed ([704 h](https://steamcommunity.com/id/RedMinjo/recommended/2471100/)),
  and a stat advisor ([489 h](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198390899163/recommended/2471100/)). **Community:** of 107, 66 name the community (Discord,
  its players, a wiki), 32 clans or guilds, 31 a market, 25 chat, 16 friends or co-op.
- **The developer is praised for answering in person** (a median of 540 hours at review): a purchase put right by its maker ([NGU](https://steamcommunity.com/id/Idealtrajectory/recommended/1147690/)),
  an account wiped on request ([Idle Champions](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198151805189/recommended/627690/)), Steam-only boards after a review
  ([Milky Way Idle](https://steamcommunity.com/id/W1ckedSickOne/recommended/3224420/)); Soda Dungeon 2's most-voted positive ([339 votes](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198015197776/recommended/946050/)).

## Proposals from this file

No praised thing lacks a proposal, so no new number. Evidence for existing ones:

- **P-068, reshaped** (Change; S): the likeness draws about 10 praises per fault (1.4 in Milky Way Idle, 2.5 in Idle Clans), for a lineage with something
  added: RuneScape without the chores, Melvor with friends. Name RuneScape's skills as the lineage; lead with the full-screen fight and the weekly clan boss.
- **P-057 and P-047** (Remove, Change; S): free or fair is the second praise; in the five cash-shop games money draws 0.5 to 2.1 praises per fault, USI 24.
- **P-059, P-017, P-115, P-093** (Change; S to S–M, P-059 M with balance runs) **and P-105, P-037** (Add; M or more, S–M): respect for time leads (179, 7
  praises per fault), told in background tabs, work and time away; the Titan's three-hour bank and the lost laurel are the clocks that break it.
- **P-016 with P-111, P-043, P-110** (Add; S each): developer and update praise rises to 14% past 100 hours and is praise for a reply, yet the
  developer is named in 23% of negatives past 100 hours. A card on what changed shows the work where players are.
- **P-094, then P-013** (Add; S, then S or M+): polish draws 1.2 praises per fault, under 1 in four of five games; a save that holds earns no praise.
- **P-022, P-091, P-074, P-002** (Add, Change, Change, Add; S–M, S–M, S, S–M): USI's interface draws 14 praises per fault, for a screen that shows what is not
  in use. Fantasy Idle's measured dead stats (camp levels and attack speed past their caps) are what such a screen shows, on the thing itself.
- **P-041 and P-089** (Change, Add; S each): two of the ten most-voted positives praise play with no hands or one; the click's x2 is what they give up.
- **P-012** (Add; M) **and P-027 with P-119** (Add, Change; S, S–M): presentation draws 10 praises per fault, 0.8 in silent Melvor; 12 of 20 sound praises
  name music; humour, writing and lore are 46.
- **P-004, P-003, P-035** (Add, Add, Change; M, S–M, S): community draws 6 praises per fault; of its 107, 32 name clans or guilds, 16 friends or co-op.
- **P-079** (Change; S): under 10 hours progress is praised by 10% and the grind faulted by 12%. **P-030, P-040, P-044 under P-108** (Add; S–M each):
  collecting is a long-haul praise (39, a median of 849 hours at review).
