## In short

1. **Big on phones, small on Steam.** 一念逍遥 has 669,832 ratings on China's App Store ([store](https://itunes.apple.com/cn/lookup?id=1501671346)); Immortal Taoists and 寻道大千's
   global release have 5M+ downloads each on Google Play. On Steam the largest Idler-tagged cultivation game found is a text chat game with 3,205 reviews; this item's three have
   90, 145 and 162. The largest Chinese-made idle RPGs on Steam aren't cultivation games: Angel Legion (17,399) and Dragon Cliff (7,989).
2. **A breakthrough that can fail is the family's sore point.** On Immortal Taoists, where sold pills raise the odds, reviewers suspect rigging; a Steam launch at 9.8% of 224
   lists four failed Foundation breakthroughs in 76 hours among its faults. Fantasy Idle's gates roll no chance: a boss that holds out costs a minute. Keep.
3. **The tribulation tests survival, which no Fantasy Idle gate does.** Path of the Idle Cultivator awakens each of six body parts with a 20-strike tribulation (7 November);
   Idle Cultivation shows a tribulation's stats before the fight. Past stage 700 a Fantasy Idle run stops dying and food buys nothing (P-023). Proposal 1: a tribulation at a
   Lord's first fall. It is close to the boss laws the owner declined.
4. **Lifespan is a forced reset, and players count what rebirth takes.** xiuzhen idle resets skills each life (156 helpful); Idle Cultivation's players find nothing that speeds a
   new life (480 hours), and its sects got a switch to turn disciples' lifespans and deaths off. Immortality Idle makes what carries over a ladder of nine bloodline ranks.
   Fantasy Idle keeps everything but gold and the camp: covered; evidence for P-063.
5. **Chinese players' most helpful reviews judge the developer.** In three of the ten Chinese-made games read, 7 to 10 of the top ten reviews are about the developer, the admins
   or the official QQ group: bans for negative reviews, perks for positive ones, replies mocking critics, players removed for asking. A fourth's top review (417 helpful) faults
   rewards for joining a QQ group. Fantasy Idle's "Join the Discord" gives nothing: keep it so (P-025, P-112).
6. **The paywall has a realm's name.** The 50 most helpful US reviews of Immortal Taoists and Overmortal average 3.9 and 3.3 stars against 4.75 and 4.76 overall; about 27 and 21
   of the 50 raise paying or pay-to-win; two name Ensoulment as the realm where paying starts. A $2.99 satire of pay-to-win phone idles keeps 91.3% of 1,148. Proposal 2: put help
   at a gate on P-057's list of things never sold.
7. **"Goddess Cultivation" is 女神养成, raising goddesses:** a free gacha idle JRPG with a nudity warning, not xianxia.
8. **Nothing to borrow on spirit roots, meditation, pills, sect shops or dao trees.** Trials, the 24-hour replay, the potions, the Hall and the War Tree cover them; sects of
   computer disciples are Township-scale, which is deferred; no source read showed a sect contribution shop.

## The games

### Path of the Idle Cultivator (Wuxia dev; free, Early Access since 5 September 2026)
- **What:** a mortal gathers Qi and breaks through realms as lifespan grows, brews pills, forges, leads a sect of disciples on missions and raises divine beasts; a rebirth carries
  Soul Power upgrades, bloodlines, weapon mastery and Legacy gear; 100 achievements, eight languages, online only for Fate, challenges and boards ([details](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=5007580)).
  Its launch post on Reddit said AI wrote code (0 points, upvote ratio 0.44, 7 comments, [post](https://www.reddit.com/r/incremental_games/comments/1w8ba9j/), read through the
  [archive](https://arctic-shift.photon-reddit.com/api/posts/ids?ids=1w8ba9j)); the store page discloses nothing (same details).
- **0.5.2 (7 November):** the world map becomes the home screen; body refining in six parts, each awakened by a 20-strike tribulation; meridians as 12 channels of 9 states and 9
  keystones; 92 herbs and an 11-slot cauldron; Legacy keeps weapons and armour through rebirth ([news](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=5007580&count=50)).
- **Money:** a Supporter Pack since 11 November, $4.99: Fate Coins from playtime ×3, passive Fate drops ×2, a community buff to Qi, health, defence and attack at ×2 (×4 with the
  Patreon tier), and skins ([pack](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=5581100)). September's Fate Coin event gave Patreon supporters a larger multiplier than other players (news).
- **Scale:** 77.8% of 90, 22 up and 3 down in the last 30 days ([histogram](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviewhistogram/5007580?l=english&review_score_preference=0)); 110
  playing at about 21:40 UTC on 11 November ([API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamUserStats/GetNumberOfCurrentPlayers/v1/?appid=5007580)).
- **Praise and faults:** 6 of its 10 most helpful English reviews are positive: the theme, one of the best cultivation idlers (198 hours), a first life of over 4,000 years,
  Melvor and NGU as the yardstick. Faults: a useless guide and rebirth bugs (33 hours, 12 helpful), "idle" a misnomer (144 hours), no years taken for cooking or pills, and the
  review with the most votes (18), by a player of 0.1 hours, calls it AI slop and cites the Reddit post ([reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5007580/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english)).
- **Against Fantasy Idle:** the tribulation (Proposal 1); AI undisclosed on the store drew its most voted review (P-020); a reviewer's one tip is to switch on scientific notation (P-018).

  [price](https://store.steampowered.com/api/storesearch/?term=cultivation&cc=us&l=en)). English on its page; a Chinese translation under way through 2026.
- **Its 106 patch notes** ([news](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=3697240&count=50)): a tribulation's stats shown before its fight (29 October 2025);
  soul perks that keep the shop's and the bounties' progress through reincarnation (11 November 2025), weapon mastery and the Eternal Heart kept (3 November, 11 December);
  save export, import and a backup button (6 November 2025); a sect from Golden Core for 600,000 spirit stones (9 February 2026), which the developer said should not feel
  mandatory, and a switch for disciples' lifespans and deaths a week later; a Dao Companion (May); a $1.99 pack of looks ([pack](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=4406460));
  a sequel on Patreon (27 July). Shop upgrades cost 10,000, 600,000, 20,000,000 and 450,000,000 across its four realm tiers (2 September 2025).
- **Scale:** 78.6% of 145, the last on 3 September ([histogram](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviewhistogram/3697240?l=english&review_score_preference=0)); 53 playing
  ([API](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamUserStats/GetNumberOfCurrentPlayers/v1/?appid=3697240)).
- **Faults:** 7 of its 10 most helpful are negative, from players of 16 to 978 hours: nothing new after the second or third reincarnation (978 hours), nothing that speeds a new life

### Immortality Idle (cbradley27 on galaxy.click; open source)
- **What:** choose each day's activities; a life starts at 18 with a 30-year base lifespan, lengthened by aptitudes, spirituality, food, pills and more; at death the hero is
  reborn, and a life's gains join the aptitudes at about 1/192 ([source](https://github.com/immortalityidle/immortalityidle.github.io/blob/main/src/app/game-state/character.service.ts), lines 189, 222, 664).
  Nine bloodline ranks decide what carries over: from rank 1 the weapons, from 2 the armour, from 3 an eighth of the money, from 4 four times it, from 5 a larger lifespan from
  stats; condensing the soul core and reinforcing the meridians reset the aptitudes for lasting multipliers (same file, lines 1182–1213).
- **Scale:** 4.0 of 105 ratings, 11,751 plays, 22,154 play-hours, 203 favourites; last update 1.2.0 on 26 April 2024 ([galaxy](https://galaxy.click/play/139)); 1,515 commits, 47
  forks ([GitHub](https://github.com/immortalityidle/immortalityidle.github.io)). galaxy.click's search for cultivation finds one other game, The Mana Tree, at 5,152 play-hours ([search](https://galaxy.click/search?q=cultivation)).
- **Comments:** a lopsided build (Charisma 6,200, Toughness 60) is how it is played (21 and 10 points); a player lost on what to do next (20) ([comments](https://galaxy.click/comments/139?sort=top)).
- **Against Fantasy Idle:** what carries over as a ladder (P-063); a life on a clock doesn't carry.
