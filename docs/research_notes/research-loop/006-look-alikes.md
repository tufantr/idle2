# 006: Fantasy Idle's closest look-alikes (read 30 October 2026)

Read by a subagent from galaxy.click (pages and comments), itch.io, Steam (store, reviews, news, aggregators),
IncrementalDB and Reddit (through the Arctic Shift archive). Aggregator player and sales figures and developers' own
claims are unverified.

None of the three combines what Fantasy Idle has: a painted full-screen fight with clicking, three reset layers,
clans with a weekly boss, guest play, and nothing to buy.

## Ensis IDLE (Cerbion; galaxy.click, March 2026)

- **What:** a free browser idle in dark fantasy told in words (minimalist, text; Dark Souls the inspiration). Ash
  gathered by hovering a button, not clicking; three prestige layers; clones, a map, a fort, research, perks, relic
  collections; combat with zones, gear, modifiers, reforging, bosses and a bestiary after the first portal. English,
  German, Simplified Chinese; phones. Free, Ko-fi tips. No social side beyond a Discord.
- **Scale:** galaxy.click 30,785 plays, 3.9 stars from 209 ratings; itch.io 4.4 from 39. Updates every one to two weeks.
- **Praised:** hovering instead of clicking (three players in one thread); mid-game pacing; a responsive developer
  who answered 7 of 12 visible comments. An AI note up front (code with Copilot, art and audio human) drew no AI
  complaints.
- **Complaints:** slow, repetitive early resets before automation (the two top comments); hidden unlocks and unclear
  goals; combat detached from the rest; cost spikes; a map too big for small windows.
- Sources: [galaxy.click](https://galaxy.click/play/712), [comments](https://galaxy.click/comments/712),
  [itch.io](https://cerbion.itch.io/ensis-idle), [IncrementalDB](https://www.incrementaldb.com/game/ensis-idle?id=10409).

## Scapewatch: Idle MMO (one developer; Steam, 25 September 2026)

- **What:** an online RuneScape-style idle MMO on Steam, the browser and Android with one account (Steam sign-in
  only). Queued tasks drawn at random, steered by weights; a hand-drawn map of 540+ places; 30 skills, 178 quests, 60 pets
  with shiny variants, 62 capes, a 1,500-entry collection log; no prestige. Clans with chat and live broadcasts
  (levels, quests, pets, deaths), raids of up to five (a solo player fills a raid with clones of themselves), PvP for the
  boards, holiday events where every player's finds raise one shared boost.
- **Money:** $14.99; a free demo to level 35 whose progress carries into the full game; packs that added offline hours.
- **Scale:** Very Positive, 88% of 562 reviews; about 2,200 players at once at its Steam peak; the developer claims 12,000
  copies in the first week (unverified). Four patches in four weeks, including a phone layout and a HUD size setting
  (50–150%); public stats posts.
- **What hurt it:** **paid offline time** (8 hours, each pack 8 more) led 6 of the 10 most helpful reviews and was
  doubled within five days (16 base, 48 with both packs); a **"no AI" claim** contradicted by players finding AI-written
  code, later admitted; a combative tone with critics.
- Sources: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4671380),
  [reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4671380/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english),
  [press kit](https://www.scapewatch.com/presskit), [Reddit, June post](https://www.reddit.com/r/incremental_games/comments/1u6z6ja/),
  [first-week stats](https://www.reddit.com/r/PBBG/comments/1wxigxc/).

## Milky Way Idle (cheze; browser 2022, Steam Early Access March 2025)

- **What:** a multiplayer RuneScape-inspired idle with a space-cow look in a mostly text interface; 10 skilling and 7
  combat skills; rules-driven combat waves, parties, dungeons, the Labyrinth; a player market, global chat with
  translation, guilds, nine languages; an Ironcow (no trading) mode. In July 2026 **Guild Trials**: each week four
  skilling and two combat trials; a member signs up and the trial runs from a snapshot of their gear beside normal play,
  using no food or potions; 23 guild buildings; shrines with personal buffs that work only in a guild; taking over from
  an inactive leader.
- **Money:** free to play; Cowbells bought for money that trade on the market for gold; a monthly pass; offline time
  capped at 10 hours unless bought.
- **Scale:** English reviews 72% of 768; all languages about 49% of 2,464, two-thirds not in English; peak 3,310 at once.
- **What hurt it:** **pay-to-win** (the theme of 7 of the 10 most helpful English reviews); moderation and the developer's
  attitude (9 of the 10 top Chinese reviews); a market dominated by early players and gold sellers; ban waves of 500+
  main accounts.
- Sources: [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3224420), [wiki](https://milkywayidle.wiki.gg/),
  [Cowbell Store](https://milkywayidle.wiki.gg/wiki/Cowbell_Store),
  [MooPass thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3224420/discussions/0/600776571073120773).

Glenwich Idle MMO (Steam Early Access April 2026, guilds, parties, a market; 36% of 44); Vanya Online (dark-fantasy

Melvor-likes.

## What it means for Fantasy Idle

- **Where Fantasy Idle is ahead:** its look (the others are text and icons), guest play beside accounts, nothing to
  buy, 24 hours offline free (against 16 and 10), full control of what the hero does, a clan boss with no set times.
- **The clearest warning is about money.** The two online look-alikes were hurt most by selling offline time and
  power; cosmetics drew little fire. The owner plans to sell power later (ROADMAP, Decisions 2). The evidence says that
  is where these games lost their reviews.
- **Honesty about AI is cheaper before anyone asks.** Ensis's upfront note drew no complaints; Scapewatch's denial
  became the story (P-020 gains weight).
- **Social life without set times works:** broadcasts, seeing others, raids a solo player can fill, shared event
  meters, trials that run from a snapshot beside normal play.
- **Languages matter more than expected:** two-thirds of Milky Way Idle's reviews aren't English; Ensis ships three.

## Proposals from this file

- **If power goes on sale, never offline time, XP or a currency that trades for power** (input to the owner's later
  plan, not a build): the two look-alikes that sold these lost their reviews over it; cosmetics drew little fire.
- **Clan trials from a snapshot** (shapes P-004): each week a few skilling and combat trials; a member signs up and the
  trial runs from a snapshot of their hero beside their own play, without supplies; the clan earns levels. No set times.
- **Clanmates in the hall** (Add, S–M): members' heroes, in their looks, capes and cloaks, drawn from their saves,
  standing in the Clan Hall's painting beside the feed (P-003).
- **A shared meter in weekend events** (Add, S–M): every player's progress fills one server meter that raises a small
  bonus for all, as Scapewatch's holiday finds do.
- **Collector goals without power** (Add, S): a rare shiny form of each pet and a trimmed form of each cape, counted in
  the completion figure (P-005).
- **Hold to strike, as an accessibility setting** (Change, S; reopens a deliberate choice): holding the mouse or Space
  strikes at the rate a macro already gets; the click and its combo stay. Players praise games that spare their hands
  (Ensis's hovering); the owner already treats a macro as fair play, so this gives every player what a macro gives.
- **Translations** (Add, M–L): with a native speaker checking, Simplified Chinese and German first; the game is
  English only today.
- **Rules, reports and an appeal path before the game grows** (Add, S): a report button for clan names and
  descriptions, short rules, a way to appeal. Keep chat out.
- **A monthly "state of the realm"** (Add, S, the owner's posts): server totals (monsters slain, deepest stage, most
  trained skill, pets found) as a Discord post; Scapewatch's players asked for more of these.
- **A guest's save on the server from the first minute** (Change, M; reopens a deliberate choice): under an anonymous
  key with the same growth checks, so an account made later keeps the hero, as Scapewatch carries its demo forward.
- **For the queue:** a Steam path (store page, playtest, Next Fest, launch), timed against November's launches; Lowbar
  Legends after its 13 November launch.
