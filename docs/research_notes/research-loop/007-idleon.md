# 007: Legends of IdleOn in 2025–2026 (read 30 October 2026)

Read by a subagent from idleon.wiki and other wikis and guides, Steam (store, review histogram, charts), the App Store,
the developer's changelogs and Reddit (removed posts through the Arctic Shift archive). Guides' numbers and the
allegations about the developer are unverified.

## What it is now

- **Seven worlds** (World 7 in parts: November 2025 and February 2026), 20 skills, 23 classes; up to 11 characters
  (the 11th at account level 5,000, May 2026). Players run ten because each works its own map or skill at once, family
  bonuses take the best of each class, and many systems use characters as workers.
- **Power is almost all permanent and account-wide:** stamps (120, each dropped by particular monsters, levelled with
  coins and materials on flattening curves), statues (deposit copies; x2 at 20,000, x3 at 1,000,000), alchemy, meals
  (permanent bonuses), the lab, sailing, divinity, farming, an Upgrade Vault of 90+ coin upgrades, and a Tome: one
  account score from many counters with diminishing returns and percentile titles
  ([wiki index](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Main_Page), [Stamps](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Stamps), [Statues](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Statues)).
- **Offline without a time cap:** each character banks its own away time, paid out when played next; the limits are
  inventory space and a low starting rate (fighting while away begins at 20% of active, raised by about a dozen sources
  toward 85%). Time Candies grant hours at once, from events or for gems ([AFK](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Game_Mechanics_AFK)).
- **Cards are the bestiary:** one per monster; an unowned card drops far more often; stars 0–6 on a steep copy curve
  (Green Mushroom 1/5/15/25/80/2,295/73,225), each star adding the same bonus; the top stars opened only by later content;
  sets per zone; card slots, more of them for gems ([Cards](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cards)).
- **Events:** six or seven a year, two to three weeks each; the event shop sells permanent account bonuses, four, then a
  hidden four, then four more; **points carry over between events and stock returns later**
  ([Event Shop](https://www.digitaltq.com/wiki/idleon/event-shop)). A Weekly Battle: one boss for everyone, five
  difficulties, one restart a day, trophies for a rotating cosmetic shop.
- **Guilds** up to 210: each member's first login of the day adds 10 points; daily and weekly tasks met by ordinary
  play (claim 5+ hours of AFK gains, beat a boss); 18 bonuses; weekly gift boxes ([Guilds](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Guilds)).
- **Money:** gems ($1.99–$19.99), packs ($4.99–$34.99, pet packs $16.99–$29.99); a **gacha for pets** since June 2023,
  with pity, pets that multiply account power (3x damage, 15x damage), and 52 of about 103 pets only from bundles or
  giveaways.
- **One developer**, about 20 updates a year; worlds every 9 to 21 months.

## How players took it (Steam, English reviews by month)

| Period | Positive |
|---|---|
| June 2023 (the pet gacha arrives) | 26% of 1,380 |
| January–October 2025 | 83% of 4,978 |
| November 2025 (1.0, World 7) | 91% of 3,029 |
| January–April 2026 | 56% of 1,760 |
| May–October 2026 | 70% of 1,873 |

([histogram](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviewhistogram/1476970?l=english&review_score_preference=0);
the link from the pets to the 2023 fall is a reading of the timing and forum threads, unverified.)

- **Praised:** depth, systems that feed each other, the amount of content, the pixel art and humour, generous offline
  play, frequent updates. Even a critical post (January 2026, 385 points) grants the depth and the offline progress.
- **Criticised:** pay-to-win (pets, bundle FOMO); overwhelm (World 7 burnout); daily check-ins grown well past ten
  minutes; "systems made to work, not really made to be played" (a 2025 critique); lag and crashes at depth; critical
  posts removed (allegations).
- **The account can only be seen outside the game:** players rely on IdleOn Toolbox (a dashboard of timers, alerts and
  goals) and Idleon Efficiency; the game added option toggles and in-game patch notes only in 2026
  ([Toolbox](https://idleontoolbox.com/), [External Resources](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/External_Resources)).

## Proposals from this file

- **Bestiary stars that give a little power** (Add, S–M): each kind's stars (today 10, 100, 1,000 defeats) add a small,
  equal bonus, more for bosses, with a set bonus for a land whose kinds all have three stars; the bestiary page shows
  it. A reason to farm a given land, from a collection that already exists. Size it so it never rivals tokens.
- **Projected gains before leaving** (Add, S): the welcome-back report exists; a line before leaving ("away 8 hours:
  about this many stages and levels") would show what the 24 hours are worth. Thin: one game's evidence.
- **For the queue:** the weekend events' carry-over (does an unspent festival token survive to the next event?); a
  score with percentile titles among players.
- **Evidence for the owner's plan to sell power:** IdleOn's pet gacha and paid power are the main theme of its slump
  (26% the month the gacha came; 56% in early 2026), joining Scapewatch's and Milky Way Idle's (006).
