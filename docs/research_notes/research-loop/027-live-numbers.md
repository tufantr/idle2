## What it means

- **The keen half and the rest.** Eight of fifteen are past stage 1,000 within three weeks, and five are below 100: the game
  holds its keenest and loses most of the others in the first days, which is where 019 (onboarding) and 018 (the guest link)
  point.
- **Growth needs a channel.** No new accounts came in the week after the post faded. 018's proposals (a guest link, a
  second post from 21 November, galaxy.click, counting where accounts come from) are the ones these numbers support.
