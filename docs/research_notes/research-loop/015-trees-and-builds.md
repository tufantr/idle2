# 015: Talent trees and build depth (read 11 November 2026)

Read: official pages and patch notes, wikis, guides (marked), Steam pages and review histograms (all languages; the per-language API
redirected today), Reddit through the Arctic Shift archive. Measured at 6ea4de0 on 12 heroes the simulator saved (`--auto --pacts --charms
--save-at`, seeds 1 to 3) at stages 1,300, 1,600, 2,000 and 2,300, after 155 to 556 hours; no live saves (they need the owner's OK).

**In short:** on the ladder the War Tree has one best build, in a fixed order: Patient Hunter, then the Blade, then the Hunt to Relentless.
15 of its 39 talents add nothing at the wall past stage 1,300, 12 of the Bulwark's 14 among them, and so do half the affixes the heroes wear.
Elsewhere a choice stays real when content asks for different answers and switching is cheap; players tire of trees of small percentages.
Fantasy Idle's Trials already shift the talents' worth, but the climbing build still wins or ties in all five measured.

## Trees elsewhere

- **World of Warcraft.** By Wrath its trees held 71 points and more than 80 talents a class ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2014-04-11-wow-archivist-talents-have-come-full-circle.html)).
  In 2010 Blizzard cut dull passives players felt forced to take: 31-point trees, 41 points in all ([blue post](https://www.bluetracker.gg/topic/20659-cataclysm-class-mastery-systems-update/)).
  At GDC 2011 Tom Chilton said more talents had not meant more choice, with 7 weak options in 10 as the example ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011-03-02-gdc-2011-tom-chilton-discusses-cataclysm-design-talent-and-zon.html)).
  Respec went 1, 5, 10 gold up to 50, so choices were taken seriously ([Engadget, 2006](https://www.engadget.com/2006/03/21/the-rising-cost-of-respecs/)); then dual
  spec, free swaps, and Dragonflight's 61 points changed for free outside combat, with loadouts ([preview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/23797209/), [notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23814291/dragonflight-pre-expansion-update-notesnow-live)).
- **Path of Exile.** 99 points from levels, 23 or 24 from quests, 20 refunds from quests, then an Orb of Regret a point. Keystones trade a
  rule for a cost: Resolute Technique takes critical strikes away, Chaos Inoculation life ([older wiki](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Passive_skill), [keystones](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Keystone)).
  Path of Exile 2 cut gold respecs by 40 to 50% in December 2024 (GGG's estimate, [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-of-exile-2/patch-notes-respec-cost)).
- **Last Epoch** refunds passives a point at a time for gold; skill points above a skill's floor are levelled again, a cost in time
  ([guide](https://maxroll.gg/last-epoch/resources/respec-guide), unverified). **Grim Dawn:** 55 devotion points, constellations gated by affinity,
  powers that ride a skill and level with use, refunds for a fee and an Aether Crystal ([official](https://www.grimdawn.com/guide/character/devotion)).
- **Diablo 4** cut respecs by up to 40% at level 100 (1.1.1, August 2023, [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/d4/news/patch-1-1-1-notes)), added
  Refund All ([GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/diablo-4-update-brings-ability-to-respec-entire-paragon-board-with-one-click)), then the
  Armory: five loadouts of gear, Paragon and skills, switched for no gold ([Blizzard, 2.1](https://news.blizzard.com/diablo4/24162193/)).
- **Melvor Idle.** A skill's tree gets a point per Abyssal level, 59 fill it, and no node can be undone ([wiki](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Skill_Tree)).
  Its real choice is the mastery pool: spend it on one action, or keep it above the 10, 25, 50 and 95% checkpoints that help the whole
  skill ([wiki](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Mastery)).
- **Tap Titans 2** has a tree per damage source: taps, heroes and the clan ship, skills, Deadly Strike ([guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/tap-titans-2/tt2-skill-tree-guide-en.html),
  unverified). Its developers called free resets "a massive success" and added reset tokens in January 2022 ([their note](https://allpatchnotes.com/g/tap-titans-2/exciting-new-features-and-free-skill-tree-resets)).
  **Clicker Heroes'** Ancients make idle, hybrid and active builds (Siyalatas pays after 60 s without a click, Juggernaut for clicking); a
  respec returns 75% ([wiki](https://clickerheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Ancients)). **IdleOn's** two talent presets serve fighting and skilling or
  AFK ([forum](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/3195866872033775166), unverified). **Idle Champions'** build is the formation:
  buffs reach the champions beside or behind ([wiki stub, outdated](https://idlechampions.fandom.com/wiki/Formation_strategy)).
- **Players.** On r/incremental_games in September 2025 (85 points), the top answers want a tree shown tier by tier, no small-percentage
  filler, and everything buyable in the end; one says a single route or a costly change makes a bad tree
  ([thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/incremental_games/comments/1nfgb75/), [archive](https://arctic-shift.photon-reddit.com/api/comments/search?link_id=1nfgb75&limit=100)).
  Lowmoor, a zero-player idle RPG with 2,108 nodes (August 2026, 190 points), spends its points itself if left alone, so a player can miss the
  tree; one wanted "a bunch of exciting game-twisting stuff" instead of +1 stats ([thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/incremental_games/comments/1vi0u3h/), archive with `link_id=1vi0u3h`).

## Depth in games that fight by themselves

- **Melvor's triangle:** the right style deals ×1.10 with ×1.25 damage reduction, the wrong one ×0.85 with ×0.75 to 0.95, reversed in the
  Abyssal Realm ([wiki](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Combat_Triangle)): each monster asks its own question. AFK Journey's Dream Realm rotates
  four bosses daily, each with its own guide team ([guide](https://gameluster.com/afk-journey-skyclops-dream-realm-boss-guide/), unverified).
- **Parties and spells cast for you:** Soda Dungeon 2's Soda Script runs a mixed party unwatched (94% of 6,238, [store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/946050/),
  [histogram](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviewhistogram/946050?l=english&review_score_preference=0)); Magic Research 2's wizards cast
  (93% of 626, [store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2864890/)); Cauldron's party sequences skills, an autobattler later (92% of 1,751,
  [store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2619650/)). Kill the Lich Idle is built on routing one resource through 300+ actions
  ([store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4522510/), via search, unverified).
- **What gets players talking:** fights that ask different things, choices that change how a fight goes rather than one number, a cheap
  switch, no filler between choices. For one hero the smallest real choice is a rule that wins one kind of fight and not another.

Ladder: 12 heroes (the two new trees 6, at 2,000 to 2,300); Brutes: 4 to 6; Swift Bosses: 3 at 2,000. The tool moves in 10-stage gates.

- **A best build: yes, on the ladder.** A search within the tree's rules finds one order: Patient Hunter for the first 5 points (4.2), the
  Blade to 41 points (20.6), then the Hunt's 25 empty points to Relentless (24.9 at 61), the Bulwark last. Hunt first trails until 61.
  The simulator's `TALENT_ORDER` already plays it.
- **Trials shift the worth, not the winner.** Under Brutes (blows stagger) Hamstring is worth 5.0 and Riposte 4.1. Under Swift Bosses' 3-s
  clock Opening Blow is worth 11.2, Patient Hunter and Deathblow 0. Fresh runs at 2,000: in Fasting bulwark31 kept him above half health but
  blade31 went 20 stages further; Faithless cost every build about 700 stages, without a death; in Glass Hero (111 to 297 deaths a run) the
  dodge line did no better than the Blade. DESIGN §3.33 also puts the Bulwark's worth in dungeons and the Titan: deep, those stood 180 to
  1,100+ stages below the heroes.
- **Pace:** the first point came between stages 700 and 1,000; then 7–8 points at 1,300, 14–16 at 1,600, 26–29 at 2,000, 33–34 at 2,300.
  Levels double in XP every 7 (`core/xp.js`): at 1.1 million XP an hour the 41st point comes near 900 hours, the 61st past 5,000, all 134
  never. The tree never nears full; its dull stretch is the Hunt's middle. The clan boss leaves the rules out (`deriveStats`).

## Defence past the cap: what it leaves dead (stages 1,300 to 2,300)

013 holds, more strongly. At their walls the heroes had 308 to 16,063 times the boss's attack in defence (the 90% cap needs 9); a boss's
blow took 0.04 to 3.2% of their health. Fresh runs: 0 deaths, 0 dishes, lowest health 97 to 100%. Blows shrink with depth, so it should hold
at 2,440 (not measured: the heroes stop at 2,300). On the ladder this leaves dead:

- **Talents:** the 15 above (Hamstring and Light Feet count in Brutes; Berserker would fire in Fasting, unmeasured).
- **Affixes:** max health, lifesteal, dodge without Riposte or Vengeance, and attack speed: every hero sat 7 to 48 points past the +100% cap,
  so the affix, Focus's +15%, Waterfall and the top of Rogue add nothing.
- **Perks,** which can't be refunded (`buyPerk`): Warlord's 50 levels (275 skill points) and Gourmet's 6. In Paragon those 275 would buy
  about 33 levels: ×1.39 attack, some 9.5 stages at 2,300.
- **Camp and the rest:** the Armour Rack and Hearth take two thirds of camp gold (levels 223/223/222; in the Whetstone, 2.3 more levels,
  about 3.3 stages). Agility's Monkey Bars, Tightrope, Rock Wall, Lava Crossing and Waterfall. Gear's defence, which counts only as health
  (`BASE.hpPerDef`); the defence and health shares of tokens, Legacy, potions and medals; food (013).
- **P-023 re-checked:** its first way (`denseAtkGrowth` 1.035) multiplies monsters' attack ×6.6 at 940 (013 said about ×3), ×38 at 1,300,
  ×1,124 at 2,000 and ×9,461 at 2,440 (`enemyBaseStats`). That fells every deep live hero at once: a deep survival wall would need a rule
  tied to the hero, not a change in growth.

## Affixes as a build system

Eight kinds (`data/items.js`): crit chance 1–3%, crit damage 5–15%, attack speed 2–6%, dodge 1–3%, lifesteal 1–3%, gold 3–8%, combat XP
2–5%, max health 2–5%, times 1 + 0.08 a tier above copper (×1.48 at tier 7). Rarity gives 0 (common) to 6 (Primal, rolled at the top),
drawn without repeats; the anvil's and bench's reroll redraws them all. Of the 490 affixes the heroes wore, 251 (51%) were the four dead
kinds, 113 (23%) gold or XP, 126 (26%) crit. One more tier-7 middle roll adds 0.86 stage (crit chance) or 0.74 (crit damage); the other
four, 0.00. As no kind repeats on a piece, at most two of a Primal's six can move the wall.

## Proposals from this file

1. **The Bulwark pays at the boss's clock** (Change, M). Defence past the cap, health or a dodge could add attack or give clock time back
   (once a boss, Second Wind could hold the clock 3 s); Berserker gets a new rule. Size it near the Blade's 14 for 31 points with a boss
   bench and `tools/talents.mjs`. Why: 12 of its 14 talents score 0.00 for all 12 heroes, the tree opens past the depth where survival
   counts, and Chilton, Blizzard's 2010 cut and both threads show what such choices cost. Risk: defence becomes the best stat; check stage 800.
2. **Capped stats spill into damage, and the cap is shown** (Change: S for the mark the owner promised on 1 November, S–M for the spill),
   then a reroll that keeps one bonus and no max health or lifesteal on drops past stage 700 (S–M). Why: 51% of 490 worn affixes, the attack
   speed past the cap on every hero and 275 skill points of perks do nothing; Diablo 3 and 4 took useless stats off loot. Keep dodge:
   Brutes, Glass Hero and the counters use it.
3. **Evidence, no new number:** P-023's first way re-checked (×38 to ×9,461 at live depths); P-022's panel would show defence and health
   at 0 deep; 004's audit row 16 (two workable builds, cheap respec) now measured: one build on the ladder.

Not carried over: loadouts and a cheaper unlearn until builds differ by content (today the climbing tree wins or ties in every Trial
measured); parties, formations and scripts (one hero); a Path of Exile-sized tree that needs planners; Grim Dawn's skill-bound powers (no
skills to bind); Melvor's triangle (ranged and magic are deferred).
