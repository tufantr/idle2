This page says what Fantasy Idle keeps about you, and why. The short version comes first, and the full notice is below it. The game is run by the Fantasy Idle developer team ("we"): ask us anything about it at [contact@fantasyidle.com](mailto:contact@fantasyidle.com).

## In short

### What we keep, and why

- Your **username**, and your **password** in a scrambled form that nobody can read back: so you can sign in.
- Your **email**: only to send you a code when you sign up, or when you forget your password. No other player sees it.
- Your **game**: your hero and everything in your save, so you can play it on any device.
- Your **clan**: which clan you are in, and the words of a clan you start. Every signed-in player can read clan names and words.
- The **leaderboards**, only if you join them: your username and your numbers.
- **Sign-in counters**: how many tries came from your internet address, to stop password guessing.

### For how long

- Your account, email and game: until you delete your account. We don't delete accounts that go quiet.
- Your clan fights and gifts, the boards' weekly numbers and the sign-in counters: no end date yet.
- Backups of all of it: about two weeks.

### Who helps us run the game

- **RackNerd** rents us the server the game runs on.
- **Cloudflare** stands in front of the site: every visit passes through it, to keep the site fast and safe.
- **Resend** sends the game's emails: your codes.
- We don't sell your data, show ads or follow you around the web.

### Where it is kept

- On our server in the United States, and in your own browser: your game's copy and your settings.

### What you can do

- **See it:** email us, and we send you a copy.
- **Change it:** set a new password with "Forgot your password?" when you sign in. To change your email or username, email us.
- **Delete it:** **Settings → Delete my account**. Leaving the leaderboards, or your clan, takes one tap.
- **Complain:** tell us, or a data protection authority (below).

### Who we are

The Fantasy Idle developer team runs the game at fantasyidle.com and this wiki, and decides what is kept and why. Reach us at [contact@fantasyidle.com](mailto:contact@fantasyidle.com). We read and answer in English.

The game doesn't ask your age, and our server doesn't record your country or where you are.

### Kept outside the database

- **Backups.** Every night at 03:30 UTC a copy of the whole database is made on the same server, readable only by the database's own system account. Each copy is kept for about two weeks, then deleted.
- **The web server's log.** Each visit to the game or this wiki is logged with your internet address, the time, the page and your browser's name. The log is cleared after about two weeks.
- **The game server's own log** holds error messages only, not a record of each visit.
- **Cloudflare** carries everything between your browser and our server, so it handles your internet address and what your browser sends and receives. Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers what it keeps.
- **Resend** gets your email address, your username (each mail greets you by name) and the code, for each mail it sends, and keeps a log of each mail for 30 days.

### Kept in your browser

- The game keeps, in your browser's storage: a copy of your game and a few backups (every 10 minutes, when the game loads, before a prestige or an Ascension, before a hard reset, and a guest's game put aside when an account signs in); your sign-in, good for 30 days; which save on our server this device saw last; and small settings, such as the tab you were on.
- Logging out takes your sign-in and your game's copy off this browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it. Some browsers clear a site's storage by themselves: Safari does after seven days without a visit.
- The game and this wiki set no cookies, and run no analytics, ads or trackers. Cloudflare may set a cookie it needs to tell people from bots.
- Everything the game and this wiki load, fonts included, comes from fantasyidle.com.
- If you turn on the playtest log in Settings, its timeline (what happened in your game, and when the game was in view) is part of your save, so it goes to our server with it.

### Who helps us, and where your data goes

- **RackNerd**, our server host: the server with the database, the backups and the logs is rented from RackNerd, and stands in the United States.
- **Cloudflare**: its network stands in front of fantasyidle.com and this wiki (the site's addresses, HTTPS and protection), with servers around the world.
- **Resend**: sends the game's mail, from its servers in Ireland. It is a US company, and may handle the data in the United States.
- **Other players** see only what the table above says.
- **The authorities**, only where the law makes us tell them: to report child sexual abuse, for example.
- We sell nothing about you, and share it with no one else. If you live outside the United States, your data goes there; where the law asks for safeguards for that, we rely on these companies' data processing terms.
- The game's Discord is Discord's own service, under Discord's privacy policy. There we see only what you post, as any member does.

### Why we may use it

- **To give you the game you signed up for:** your account, email, save, clan and clan fights.
- **Your choice:** the leaderboards. Join or leave them at any time.
- **To keep the game safe and fair:** the sign-in counters, the backups, the logs and the check on saves.
- **When the law requires it:** reports of illegal content.

### The check on saves

A program on our server checks the numbers in every save. If they grew faster than the game allows, the account is flagged: for 30 days its clan attacks are capped, its Endless Hydra fights don't count in the ranking, and it is left off the boards. A person looks again if you ask: see [[/rules#flags|Flags]]. The check reads numbers, never what you write.

### Your rights

- You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it or to object to it (to the check on saves, too), and for your save as a file. Email us, and we answer within a month.
- **Delete my account** is in Settings. It deletes your account from our server: your username, email, password and game, your place in your clan, and your entries on the boards and in clan fights. Copies in the nightly backups go when those backups do, about two weeks later. **Hard reset**, also in Settings, starts your game over (on our server too) but keeps your account.
- You can complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office, in the EU the authority of your country, in Turkey the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK). We'd like the chance to put things right first.

### Children

We don't ask anyone's age. Instead, the game works the same careful way for everyone: we keep only what the game needs, no other player sees your email or your hero's name, the leaderboards are off until you join them, and there is no chat, no private messages, no ads and no tracking. Parents can write to us about a child's account.

### Keeping it safe

Passwords and email codes are kept only as hashes. The site works only over HTTPS, and our server answers the web only through Cloudflare. The backups can be read only by the database's own account. If your data is ever exposed, we will tell you, and the authorities, as the law requires.

### Changes to this page

When we change what we keep or why, we update this page and say so on the Discord. Last changed: 12 November 2026.
