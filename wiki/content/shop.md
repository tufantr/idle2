The Shop sells supplies for gold: coal, logs, herbs, meat, bait, essence and, for [[Crafting]], gems. It opens early, after the first boss falls. Besides the supplies it holds your [[Prestige|prestige]] panel and the [[Perks]] you buy with skill points. The table below lists every supply.

## Prices follow your best stage

Every supply is priced in kills: so many kills' worth of gold at your best stage ever, where a boss stage prices like its ordinary monsters. The table below gives each price in kills, and in gold at a few best stages.

So prices rise with what your kills pay: buying stays about as dear, against what you earn, at any depth, and the shop never makes gathering pointless. Gold bonuses don't raise prices, so they make everything cheaper.

## Gem pouches

Once Crafting is open the Shop also sells gems for it: a pouch of the first gems, one kind at a time as your Crafting level climbs (the table below says which, when). They keep Crafting's first levels open at any point of the game, however deep the fight's own gems have gone. The higher gems are never sold for gold.

## No buying low and selling high

Selling pays a fixed price. Materials sell from the Inventory for a small sum set by their kind and tier ({{res('coal')}} for {{sellValue('coal')}} gold each, say), which never grows with your stage, while supplies cost kills' worth of gold at your best stage. Nothing bought here can be sold back at a profit, and [[Monster Essence]] cannot be sold at all.

Gold is the run's money: it comes from kills and goes at your next prestige, so spend it on supplies and the [[Camp]] while it lasts.
