A supply crate ripens every {{time(DAILY_INTERVAL_MS)}}, and up to {{DAILY_MAX_BANKED}} wait for you, so a missed day costs nothing and there is no streak to keep. A new hero starts with one ready.

## Opening crates

When a crate is ripe, a crate button shows at the top of the screen with the number waiting, and the welcome-back report lists it too. Open them one at a time. On a phone the crate sits in the bar at the bottom, and between crates it counts down to the next one.

While all {{DAILY_MAX_BANKED}} are waiting no more pile up, and the time toward the next one is lost: open one to start the clock again.

## What is inside

A crate is filled from your best stage ever, so it keeps pace with you (the table below gives the amounts):

- **gold:** the gold of a batch of kills at your best stage, raised by your gold bonuses;
- **materials** picked from the loot table of the land of your best stage;
- **[[Monster Essence|essence]]**, more for a richer land;
- **a gem:** the best one of that land's tier.

## The great crate

Every {{GREAT_CRATE_EVERY}}th crate you open is a great crate, with more of everything and a gem of the next tier besides (the table below). It counts the crates you open, not days in a row, so there is still no streak to lose. The crate's dialog shows how far you are from the next great one, and the crate button says when the next one is great.

> Tip: a crate's gold is run money and goes at your next [[Prestige|prestige]]. Open crates when you can spend the gold, for example on the [[Camp]] early in a run.
