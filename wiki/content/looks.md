Your hero can look the way you like. A look is a body and a hairstyle; your gear goes on top of whichever you choose, and a helmet hides the hair. Looks are only for show: they change nothing else in the game.

## Choosing a look

- On the title card, when you begin, the arrows beside your hero turn through the looks.
- Any time later, Settings shows a portrait of every look you can wear. Tap one to put it on.

{{LOOKS.filter(l => !l.medal).length}} looks are open to everyone from the start: humans, elves and a dwarf, shown below.

## Looks earned with medals

{{LOOKS.filter(l => l.medal).length}} more looks are earned, each with a [[Medals|medal]], as shown below. When you win the medal, its card shows your hero in the new look, and the look joins the others in Settings. Settings also shows the next look you could earn as a silhouette, naming the medal that brings it, except when that medal is a secret one: then the look stays a surprise.

## Name and cloak

You can name your hero too, on the title card or in Settings. The name shows over your hero in the fight and on your hero's panel in the Inventory; without one, your hero is simply "You".

The cloak on your hero's back is the colour of your [[Ranks|rank]], unless you choose to wear a [[/capes|skill cape]] or a festival cloak instead.
