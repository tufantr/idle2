Fantasy Idle is made and run by **the Fantasy Idle developer team**: "we" on this page. These are the game's rules, short and in plain words. They apply to everyone who plays with an account, and making an account means you agree to them. What we keep about you is on the [[/privacy|Privacy]] page.

## How to reach us

- **Email:** {{link(`mailto:${CONTACT_EMAIL}`, CONTACT_EMAIL)}}. We read and answer in English.
- **Discord:** the game's {{`<a href="${esc(DISCORD_INVITE)}" rel="noopener">Discord server</a>`}}. Discord is a separate service with its own rules, and you don't need it to reach us.
- You don't need an account to write to us.

## Clan names and words

Every clan has a name, a tag, a description and a "looking for" line, and **every signed-in player can read them**. They must not hold:

- anything against the law;
- slurs, or hate against people for who they are;
- threats, bullying or harassment;
- sexual content;
- anything that encourages hurting yourself, anyone else or animals, or describes it in gory detail, such as suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, violence, drugs or dangerous challenges;
- anyone's personal details, such as a real name, where someone lives or goes to school, or a phone number;
- contact details or invitations to other apps and sites: no handles, links or invite codes. You may name the game's own Discord;
- pretending to be someone else: another player, our team, or a real person or company;
- ads, or anything for sale.

Your **username** follows the same rules.

A clan's owner can change its description and "looking for" line at any time, under the same rules. The game itself refuses links, handles, invite codes and other contact details in those two lines.

## What we do

- **We read every new clan ourselves.** A person on our team judges what players write, not a program. The one check that runs by itself is the game's refusal of links, handles, invite codes and other contact details in a clan's description and "looking for" line. (A [[/rules#flags|flag]] is a program's check on the numbers in saves, never on words.)
- If a clan's words or a username break these rules, we may **edit or clear the words, rename the clan, or change the username**.
- We may **close an account** that breaks the rules badly, or again and again.
- Anything against the law is taken down as soon as we see it. Anything that sexually exploits a child is taken down at once and reported to the authorities.
- When we act, we email the player concerned (for a clan, its owner): what we did, why, and how to ask us to look again, unless the law stops us. We keep a note of what we changed and why, so that it can be put back.

## Reporting a clan

If a clan's name or words break these rules, tell us:

1. Press **Report** on the clan's row in the Clan tab, which starts an email to us with the clan named, or email {{link(`mailto:${CONTACT_EMAIL}`, CONTACT_EMAIL)}} yourself. You can tell us on the Discord too, but email keeps it private.
2. Say **which clan** (its name and tag), **what is wrong**, and that what you tell us is true as far as you know.
3. You can report without an account, even if you don't play.

What happens next:

- We reply to say we have your report.
- We look at the clan, usually within a few days, and at once if someone could get hurt.
- We tell you what we decided, and why.

> Warning: if someone is in danger right now, call your local emergency number first. If something you saw upset you, tell an adult you trust as well.

## Appeals

Think we got something wrong? It might be a report you made, a change to your clan or username, a closed account or a flag. **Reply to our email and tell us why.**

- We look at it again and tell you what we decided.
- If we were wrong, we put things back as they were, as far as we can.
- We may set aside messages that are plainly not genuine, such as the same complaint sent over and over.

## Flags

The game's server checks every save it receives. If a save holds **numbers no save can reach** (an attack far beyond what its best stage allows, say), the account is **flagged**. A program decides this, not a person.

A number that only grew **faster than the game allows** is never flagged by the program. It goes on a list for a person on our team, who looks at it and may flag the account by hand.

A flag doesn't stop you playing or saving, and Settings says until when it lasts. For 30 days after the last flagged save:

- each of your [[/clans|clan]] attacks deals at most 1% of the boss's health (its card says "Flagged: at most 1% of the boss");
- your fights at the Endless Hydra don't count in your clan's ranking (their card says "Flagged: not counted in the ranking");
- you are left off the leaderboards, and out of your clan's measure of clan power.

Think a flag is wrong? Email {{link(`mailto:${CONTACT_EMAIL}`, CONTACT_EMAIL)}} with your username and how you played. A person looks at your save, and if the flag was wrong, we clear it.

## Your account

- An account needs an **email**: we send a code there when you sign up, and another if you forget your password. We also send a short note if your password changes, if someone tries to sign up with your address, or if we act on your clan, your username or your account. We send it no news or ads.
- **You can delete your account** at any time: **Settings → Delete my account**, with your password. It deletes your account and your game from our server at once. What you gave your clan stays with it, with no name on it, and backups already made lose your account only as they expire: the [[/privacy#your-rights|Privacy]] page has the details. A guest's game lives only in that browser.
- What we keep, and for how long, is on the [[/privacy|Privacy]] page.

We may change these rules as the game grows. When we make a big change, we say so on the Discord and on this page. Last changed: 19 November 2026.
